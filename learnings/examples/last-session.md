# Last Session...

Examples for the history heading: which events are told, how gains are told, and how event headlines are worded. It also holds the condensed Revelation examples. Revelations were a separate matter until the unified pipeline; what was learned is now told here as an event, or held as a fact under an open heading (PREF-005). Conventions are in the [README](../README.md#conventions).

### EX-079 Dunk's visit to Farmer Gill disappears as a historical event
- Verdict: missing (pass 8), then good inclusion (pass 9) · Session 001, unified passes 8, 9 and 12 · SRC-57, SRC-60
- Rule: [PREF-039](../preferences.md#pref-039). Allowance: [ALW-006](../allowances.md#alw-006)

**Pass 8 history** (verbatim, complete):
> The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map of places they did not recognize.
> Eric the Reeve offered a bounty for the black dragon. Sir Phidipaldi then insulted Eric and challenged him to a duel, which Eric declined.
> Bentley, Farmer Gill and Quinn said supplies have stopped reaching Brandonsford, leaving the taverns and smith short.
> Quinn hired Sir Phidipaldi to get back at Bentley, whom Quinn blames for stealing his ale.

Dunk spent the evening drinking with Gill, who told him about the dragon, his sons, Warwick and Ingrid (beats 26–32). The encounter's information was spread across other threads, and its own candidate was framed as an ordinary mule purchase rated low/low, so code dropped it.

**User's words:**
> we lost any mention of dunk and farmer gill. Most of that is fine, but seems to deserve a development at least.

> For Dunk and Farmer Gill, is there a general fix that won't be specific to this session that we can make?

> Can your add the presence of the dunno gill thread as a development in session 001 as a learning

("dunno gill" is the Dunk/Gill encounter.) Pass 9's history included:
> Dunk heard Farmer Gill's legend of Sir Brandon and kept him drinking on his porch all evening.

**Pass 12** (verbatim): "Dunk drank with Farmer Gill, who offered him a mule and told how Sir Brandon once slew a dragon." The agent proposed removing the mule offer; the user: "I am actually fine with this either way." (ALW-006)

**Why:** a meaningful social encounter can deserve an event even when its incidental purchase is not a goal and its information is spread across threads. The mule stays hidden as a goal.

**Spot it:** does a supported encounter vanish as an event because its candidate is framed only around an incidental purchase?

**Outcome:** the general encounter-coverage remedy was approved (SRC-57) and implemented in pass 9; no character quota or named exception.

### EX-048 What a gained ally is for, and what their help costs
- Verdict: good (golden) · Session 002 golden, user-authored · SRC-01, R-01
- Rule: [PREF-038](../preferences.md#pref-038)

**The text** (verbatim):
> - Squints has signed on with the party.
> - Dunk has an undiluted sample of faun wine that is supposed to make almost anything drunk or fall asleep.
> - Ingrid owes Dunk for keeping her secret.

**Why:** each line is a gain, said once. The wine keeps the one property that makes it useful and its uncertainty ("supposed to"). A favour owed to the party is an asset, not a promise.

**Spot it:** a gain plus, at most, the property that makes it useful.

### EX-001 Supply problem restated as mystery progress
- Verdict: bad · Session 001, 2026-10-25 · SRC-33
- Text (verbatim): "Brandonsford's supply problem is broader than one late shipment: Farmer Gill says nothing has come into town in months, and Quinn says no real shipments have arrived in a while."
- User's words: "the supply disruption is more then one large shipment"; "my feeling is that all of these represent way too little progress towards resolving the mystery to count as a revelation"
- Still applies: a finding that only says how big, long or frequent a problem is reduces no uncertainty. A prompt clarification did not stop it; a code threshold on a classification did ([APP-001](../approaches.md#app-001)).

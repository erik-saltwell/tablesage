Examples for the obstacles heading. Conventions are in the [README](../README.md#conventions). The rules are [PREF-034](../preferences.md#pref-034) and [PREF-042](../preferences.md#pref-042). See also EX-081 (an obstacle to a method, not the end), EX-067 (scene-local confrontation), EX-023 and EX-028 in [across-the-summary.md](across-the-summary.md).

### EX-060 An unexplained refusal stated as the obstacle
- Verdict: good (the target form) · Session 001, unified pass 5, 2026-10-30 · SRC-55
- Rule: [PREF-034](../preferences.md#pref-034). Allowance: [ALW-008](../allowances.md#alw-008)

**The text** (verbatim, the only obstacle):
> Gill says Warwick always refuses to sell the mithril chain shirt he displays in his shop.

**User's words:**
> Impediments:
>   - this is good, and what i am looking for in an impediment, add that to your learnings

**Why:** it names the blocking condition with its source first, adds no fix and no speculation, and does not turn the refusal's reason into a question (EX-015).

**Spot it:** one sentence naming the blocking condition and its source, with no fix and no "why". It needs a live shirt pursuit: without one, its absence is correct (ALW-008).

### EX-037 An untaken solution recorded as an obstacle
- Verdict: bad · Session 003 Goal Impediments, 2026-10-24 · SRC-29
- Rule: [PREF-034](../preferences.md#pref-034)

**The text** (verbatim):
> The Black Dragon's scales can't be pierced, and reportedly only Sir Brandon's magic sword can kill it. The party had not yet obtained the sword, which was buried at Brandon's Barrow Mound.

It was marked resolved once the sword was taken.

**User's words:**
> Let's talk about Brandon's sword and impenetrable scales. I think those are the same impediment for the dragon goal, but the sword one is not an impediment to a goal, it is an opportunity to overcome an impediment.

**Spot it:** is it the condition that blocks the goal, or the absence of a known fix? Does gaining a possible fix "resolve" it before anyone knows it works?

# Your Goals

Examples for the goals heading. Conventions are in the [README](../README.md#conventions). The rules are [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033) (an end, the outcome only), [PREF-051](../preferences.md#pref-051) (lifecycle), [PREF-053](../preferences.md#pref-053) (significance) and [PREF-055](../preferences.md#pref-055) (order).

## End, Not Means

### EX-035 Means listed as goals, with progress notes attached
- Verdict: bad → good · Session 002 combined Campaign Matters, 2026-10-18 to 19 · SRC-07
- Rule: [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033)

**Before** (verbatim):
> - Retrieve Sir Brandon's magic sword from the Barrow Mound, about three miles north through the forest.
> - Kill the black dragon. Sir Phidipaldi has vowed to bring his lance against it.
> - Dunk: Steal the magical mithril chain shirt from Warwick's smithy. Ingrid is making a sleeping potion for Warwick from faun wine that Dunk secretly watered down to 20 percent. She told Dunk to come back tomorrow, and Dunk said he'd see her tonight.
> - Dunk: Buy Seamus the mule from Farmer Gill. Gill turned down 20 gold but says he'll drop the price once his sons come back.
> - Find the treasure on the party's treasure map. Father William has marked the Barrow Mound and the witch's home on it.

**After** (verbatim):
> - Find the treasure on the map.
> - Kill the black dragon for Eric the Reeve's 1,000-gold bounty.
> - Dunk is considering buying Farmer Gill's mule Seamus.
> - Dr. Uriah is considering hiring Drop Dead Ned.

**The Session 001 golden** (verbatim, user-authored):
> - Find the treasure in Brandonsford.
> - Kill the dragon and collect 1,000 gold.
> - Dunk is considering buying Seamus the mule from Farmer Gill.

**User's words:**
> the purchase of seamus does not have an obvious utility to achieving either the collection of treasure or the defeat of the dragon, so it is worth inclusion.   acquiring Sir Brandon's sword, while a much bigger part of the quest, is a means to kill the dragon, so it should not be included

**Spot it:** would the party still want this if every other goal were achieved? If not, it is a means. One outcome per line, no history, routes or progress.

**Since:** "is considering" was later rejected (EX-052), and the mule is now hidden as incidental (EX-072).

### EX-036 Whether the mithril shirt is its own goal flips between runs
- Verdict: borderline (an ambiguity the user accepts) · Session 002 Goals validation runs, 2026-10-21 · SRC-21, SRC-58
- Rules: [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033), [PREF-046](../preferences.md#pref-046). Allowance: [ALW-003](../allowances.md#alw-003)

**User's words:**
> Why do you think Warwick's magical mithril chain shirt is missing from run one and two?

Later (2026-11-01):
> There is an interesting discussion here about whether or not getting Warwick's shirt is itself its own goal or only a means to help them in fighting the dragon. But I actually think this is an ambiguous case, and if you assume that Warwick and, wait, and if the pipeline judges Warwick's shirt as being its own goal, then I think these two bullets make total sense.

**Spot it:** for an item plausibly both an end and a means, either classification is acceptable; flag only instability you can see within one run's output.

### EX-081 A proposed method is mistaken for the desired end, then given too much significance
- Verdict: bad · Sessions 001–002, unified pass 11 · SRC-59
- Rules: [PREF-053](../preferences.md#pref-053), [PREF-034](../preferences.md#pref-034), [PREF-051](../preferences.md#pref-051)

**The text** (verbatim, Session 001):
> Duel Eric the Reeve at dawn, if Sir Phidipaldi still means to press his challenge.
> Eric the Reeve, offended by Sir Phidipaldi's false claims and glove slap, never accepted the dawn duel.

**User's words:**
> the goal here is not to have a duel, it is to restore his honor.  also i think this is too minor and not enough narrative impact (I mean that achieving the goal does not provide much narrative impact).   Also the 'quinn did not accept' is a bad obstacle becuase it does not really block the goal of restoring his honor, and the goal should be filtered out as being not enough narrative impact

("quinn did not accept" refers to the displayed Eric nonacceptance obstacle.) The user supplied the end here; this does not mean every challenge implies an honor goal.

**Spot it:** if the proposed method were unavailable, would the character still want a broader result? Would achieving it materially change later fiction? Does the obstacle actually constrain it?

**Outcome:** pass 12 omits the duel goal and obstacle in both Sessions.

## The Outcome Only

### EX-051 The treasure goal tied to the map
- Verdict: bad; the pass 12 recurrence judged very minor · Session 001, unified passes 4–6 and 12 · SRC-55, SRC-60
- Rule: [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033)

**The text** (verbatim): "Find the treasure shown on the party's treasure map."

**The golden** (verbatim, user-authored): "Find the treasure in Brandonsford."

**User's words** ("down" is a dictation slip):
> it says the treasure is down [shown] on the map, but the map does not say where the treasure is. Also this whole map reference is unnecessary

Pass 12 recurrence, "Find the treasure marked on the party's treasure map.":
> In general terms, I prefer that we only describe the goal, although I find its inclusion here to again be a very minor issue. It is more the general case that I am nervous about.

**Spot it:** remove every lead, clue and source from the goal. Is what remains the outcome, and does it claim nothing play didn't establish?

**Outcome:** not fixed through pass 12 by explicit "no lead" rules; the false claim went in pass 5, the map reference stayed.

### EX-052 "Is considering" in goals
- Verdict: bad (reverses the golden's "is considering" form) · Session 001, unified pass 4, 2026-10-30 · SRC-55
- Rules: [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033), [PREF-051](../preferences.md#pref-051)

**The text** (verbatim):
> The party is considering killing the black dragon and dragging its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
> Dunk is considering acquiring Farmer Gill's prize mule, Shamus. Gill says it would let Dunk start his own farm.

**User's words:**
> the multiple 'is considering' lines are unnecessary. If they don't pursue the goal, it will fall off next time. This is not the place to track how accepted it is

**Spot it:** does a goal say "is considering", "may" or otherwise rate the party's commitment? State the outcome alone.

**Outcome:** fixed in pass 5 by removing the commitment field and writer rule.

### EX-058 A motive attached to a goal
- Verdict: bad · Session 001, unified pass 5, 2026-10-30 · SRC-55
- Rule: [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033)

**The text** (verbatim): "Get Farmer Gill's prize mule Shamus so Dunk can start his own farm."

**User's words:**
> Seamus: I don't think that the adventurer/wanderer seamus really wants to start a farm.  In general, we should not be including an explanation of "Why" in goals

**Spot it:** does the goal contain "so", "because", "to" or "in order to" followed by a reason? A reward that defines the outcome may stay; a reason may not.

**Outcome:** fixed in pass 6.

## Lifecycle and Significance

### EX-070 Dropped challenges and expired appointments are not live goals
- Verdict: bad · Sessions 001–002, unified pass 7; recurred in pass 11 · SRC-57, SRC-59
- Rule: [PREF-051](../preferences.md#pref-051)

**The text** (verbatim):
> Session 001: Fight Eric the Reeve in Sir Phidipaldi's duel at dawn.
> Session 002: Fight Eric the Reeve in the dawn duel Sir Phidipaldi challenged him to.

**User's words:**
> "Fight Eric" was dropped by the party and Sir Phidipaldi.   Sir Phidipaldi issued the challenge, but it was not accepted and Sir Phidipaldi dropped it.

> Eric the reeve duel - it has clearly gotten past the next morning, so the duel is now obselete because the time has passed.

> 1) approved.  fyi, there is no explicit 'sir phidipaldi wont do this' but the next day comes and he does not go.   given that the reeve never agreed it was unlikely to ever have lasted beyond the scene anyway.

Pass 11 recurrence, Session 002 (beside the duel goal in EX-081):
> Eric the Reeve has not accepted Sir Phidipaldi's dawn duel. He is offended by Sir Phidipaldi's false claims about George's dragon and the glove slap.

> yes this needs to be fixed

See EX-081 for the end-versus-method reading.

**Spot it:** is the only support an unaccepted challenge or old resentment, after elapsed time or abandonment? Do not require an explicit withdrawal.

**User's words:**

### EX-071 Put adventure goals before immediately actionable side goals
- Verdict: bad ordering · Session 002, unified passes 7, 11 and 12 · SRC-57, SRC-59, SRC-60
- Rule: [PREF-055](../preferences.md#pref-055)

**The text** (verbatim, pass 7):
> Acquire the mithril chain shirt from Warwick the smith's shop.
> Find the treasure marked on the party's treasure map.
> Kill the black dragon and drag its body into Brandonsford to collect Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.

**User's words:**
> If there is an importance or narrative impact field, we should sort by that from highest to lowest, or sort the adventure goals first, or something

Pass 11, Session 002 (verbatim, both rated adventure/high):
> Find the treasure the party seeks.
> Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.

> is it possible the ordering can be done in deterministic code?

Pass 12 listed the treasure before the dragon hunt (both rated primary adventure). Asked whether to "put the adventure currently driving the party's choices first", the user replied: "Yes"

**Spot it:** does an easily actionable side goal, or an adventure the party isn't currently pursuing, come before the adventure driving their choices?

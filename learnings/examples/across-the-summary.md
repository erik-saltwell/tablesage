# Across the Summary

Examples that span headings: restatement and placement across headings, wording and presentation, and the user's handmade golden. Conventions are in the [README](../README.md#conventions).

Most duplication examples (EX-003, EX-021 to EX-025) come from the 2026-10-28 Brandonsford regeneration in the port's deployment workspace, recorded verbatim in the duplication analysis (`.work-items/reduce-session-matters-duplication/analysis.md`, SRC-53). They were judged before the unified pipeline, under the old per-Matter headings; the patterns still apply to the unified headings.

## Restatement and Placement

### EX-003 Open bargain restated across categories (Quinn)
- Verdict: bad · Session 001, port deployment, 2026-10-28 · SRC-53, SRC-55
- Rules: [PREF-004](../preferences.md#pref-004), [PREF-015](../preferences.md#pref-015)

**The text** (verbatim):
> Development: "Sir Phidipaldi agreed to help Quinn get back at Bentley for 100 gold by staging a fake ale shipment."
> Goal: "Help Quinn get back at Bentley for 100 gold."
> Opportunity: "Quinn will bring bottles for a fake ale shipment. If Sir Phidipaldi fills them with water and makes a show of delivering them, they could see who takes Quinn's ale."

The Mystery in the same Summary, "Who or what empties Quinn's ale at the Golden Egg every night?", is distinct and should stay.

**User's words:**
> I mean the overlap is legitimate.  This does not mean the duplication isn't a cost, but that there are good reasons to keep in both Developments and at least one Open Matters.  So in a way i am saying that it is both a problem and not a problem, if that makes sense.

**Why:** the reader meets the same unfinished deal three times; the goal adds nothing to the event.

**Spot it:** for each unresolved two-sided deal told in history, does another heading restate the *whole deal*, rather than only its own side (the task's outcome, the price, a way to carry it out)?

### EX-024 Open bargain restated across categories (Warwick)
- Verdict: bad (mixed: some entries add distinct facts) · Session 004, port deployment, 2026-10-28 · SRC-53
- Rule: [PREF-004](../preferences.md#pref-004)

**The text** (verbatim, with the analysis's relationship notes):
> Development: "The party promised to bring Warwick metal from the Dwarven mine in return for his mithril shirt." (original)
> Goal: "Bring metal from the Dwarven mine back to Warwick the smith." (restates the Development)
> New Asset: "Warwick's mithril chain shirt, lent to the party." (partial: adds that it is a loan)
> Opportunity: "The cave-in at the Dwarven mine looks diggable. If the party digs through it, they could reach metal for Warwick." (partial: mirrors the Impediment)
> Obligation: "The party must return Warwick's borrowed mithril chain shirt." (distinct: the price side)
> Goal Impediment: "A cave-in seals the Dwarven mine entrance. It blocks the party from bringing metal back to Warwick." (distinct)

**Spot it:** mark each bullet in a cluster as original, restates or distinct. More than one "restates" is the failure.

### EX-021 One threat spread across every threat category
- Verdict: bad · Session 003, port deployment, 2026-10-28 · SRC-53
- Rules: [PREF-004](../preferences.md#pref-004), [PREF-042](../preferences.md#pref-042)

**The text** (verbatim):
> Development: "A huge undead skeleton with a greatsword blocked the doorway of Sir Brandon's tomb."
> Present Danger: "A seven-foot brown skeleton in rusted armor and a mustachioed death mask stands in the tomb's doorway, dragging a huge greatsword…"
> Goal Impediment: "A huge undead skeleton with a greatsword roams the Barrow Mound. It stands in the way of clearing the mound's evil for the goblins."
> Brewing Trouble: "The huge undead skeleton in the tomb doorway may attack the party."
> Brewing Trouble: "The undead skeleton may kill more Free Goblin Society members in the Barrow Mound."
> Mystery: "Why does a huge undead figure with a greatsword roam the Barrow Mound?"

**User's words** (SRC-51, which led to the analysis):
> some kind of developments seem to show up in many part session matters, and they don't seem to add any extra information. They are just things that could apply in multiple matters, so the llm phrases it in ways appropriate for different matters and they die up all the place.

**Why:** six bullets about one creature, four of which say "there is a dangerous skeleton". The stopping situation may repeat other headings (PREF-017), but the threat headings should not repeat each other.

**Spot it:** a threat live at the stopping point appears once as danger. Other headings may add only a distinct link, such as which goal it blocks.

### EX-026 The user's own golden: one subject, several angles

### EX-056 Quinn's bargain under the unified definitions: no goal, and two non-debts as debts
- Verdict: bad, plus a missing goal · Session 001, unified pass 4, 2026-10-30 (facts `fact_19` to `fact_21`) · SRC-55
- Rules: [PREF-036](../preferences.md#pref-036), [PREF-020](../preferences.md#pref-020), [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033), [PREF-035](../preferences.md#pref-035)

**The text** (verbatim "Promises and debts"):
> Quinn will pay Sir Phidipaldi 100 gold for helping him get back at Bentley.
> Sir Phidipaldi must stage a showy fake ale delivery of water-filled bottles at the Golden Egg with Quinn.

Under "Your goals": nothing about Quinn. The pass 4 rule recorded a bargain's task as `owe`, never `want`.

**User's words** ("owl" is a dictation slip):
> there is no goal about catching quinns thief

> this is a section for the party making a Promise or owing a debt, not situations where somebody owes a debt or makes a promise to the party, which is what the first bullet is.

> The second bullet is also not a promise or a debt; it is actually an opportunity or a technique for uncovering who is stealing the owl [ale], which is a goal

**Spot it:**
- Does every "Promises and debts" bullet say what the party owes? What someone owes the party is misplaced.
- Is a plan or method listed as a promise? It is an opening.
- When the party has taken up a job, is its outcome a goal?

**Outcome:** pass 5 recorded a paid job as a `want` with its reward and the method as an opening, but the goal kept the deal's framing ("Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect his promised 100 gold."). Recurred in pass 11 ("Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect Quinn's 100 gold."), which the user judged "worth fixing" (SRC-59). Pass 12's Session 001 names catching the thief.

### EX-049 The user's first handmade Campaign Matters
- Verdict: good (the original target; several choices later changed) · Session 002, hand-written 2026-10-17 to 18 · R-10, SRC-01
- Rules: [PREF-004](../preferences.md#pref-004), [PREF-007](../preferences.md#pref-007), [PREF-033](../preferences.md#pref-033)

> Fine the treasure in brandonsford
> Kill the dragon and collect your 1000 gold
>
> ## obligations
> - give squints a share of your loot and 3 adventuring tips a day
> ## threats
> - the dragon will soon be able to fly
> - the town is running out of supplies
>
> ## opportunities
> If you get Brendan's sword, it can help slay the dragon.
> If you can steal Warwicks chain mail, it will protect you against the dragon
> If you get the portion from ingrid, it will keep Warwick asleep while your steal the chain mail

**Added 15 minutes later** (verbatim):
> If you hire the other adventurers they can help you kill the dragon
> If you free Naggeneen, he'll give you his pot of gold or a secret
> ## Revelations
> Sir Brandon's sword location is known
> Naganeen the clurichen was the one stealing booze from the Golden Egg
> The dragon has a poisonous bite and spits sulfur, and no weapon has pierced its scales.
> Ingrid loves Warwick the Smithy and has been leaving items by his bed.
> Eric the Reeve may not have the money.

**What it shows:** about ten words per entry, one point each; questions come first and include planted oddities and situations that went quiet; goals are ends only; openings use "If you X, Y"; threats are clocks; doubt sits on the premise ("supposed to have killed", "may not have the money"); one subject appears under several headings. The generated outputs of the time had 650–730 words against its 145.

**Changed since:** the window and potion are parts of one plan (PREF-045); a goal-less reward offer is not an opening (EX-041); Revelations no longer exist as a heading (PREF-005); Gill's sons are not required (ALW-007).

**Spot it:** set a new Summary beside it. Is each bullet this short, in this voice, under the heading whose question it answers?

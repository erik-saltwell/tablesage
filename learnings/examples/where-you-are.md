- Rule: [PREF-056](../preferences.md#pref-056)

> Sir Phidipaldi is at the bar of the empty Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm, drinking with Gill on the porch before bedding down in the barn.

### EX-077 The stopping situation should include an established immediate plan
- Verdict: missing · Session 002, unified pass 7 · SRC-57
- Rule: [PREF-056](../preferences.md#pref-056)

**The text** (verbatim):
> The party is in the field near the giant's cyclopean cottage in the woods north of Brandonsford. Squints and the freed Brother Dirk are with them.

Play had established the decision to walk to the Barrow; Dirk wanted to go home and the party objected.

**User's words:**
> They have an immediate goal of going to the barrow (or maybe of convincing brother dirk to come with)

> 8) approved, if there is one

**Spot it:** does the reader know what action or conversation play resumes with, without the Summary inventing a plan or an agreement?

**Outcome:** fixed in pass 8. Later passes added a secondary hierarchy detail to the same sentence (EX-078 recurrence).

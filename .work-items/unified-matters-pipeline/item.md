# Unified Matters Pipeline

Rebuild everything after the Ledger as one pipeline that models the Session as threads and their facts. It produces the Matters JSON, the Table Recap and the Session Summary as views of that model, so each fact is recorded once and duplication is prevented by design rather than cleaned up afterwards.

This supersedes [Reduce duplication across Matters within a Session](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/) and its pattern items P1 to P6, closed on 2026-10-29. Their [duplication analysis](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/analysis.md) is this item's evidence.

See [Intent](intent.md) for the framing, the proposed pipeline, the constraints, the unresolved questions and the quality rubric. The rubric was revised for this approach and approved by the user on 2026-10-29. Decisions approved the same day: threads as the model's unit, facts recorded by meaning rather than heading, New Assets and Revelations folded into Developments and forward-looking facts, and Obligations kept as the `owe` fact kind. Step 1 produces scenes with beats and a cue for each scene; threads point at beats, and the Session cue is chosen from the scene cues while writing the Summary. The new outputs are written to a `unified/` subfolder of each Session, hidden from the app, by a hidden `tablesage generate-unified-matters` command that regenerates Sessions from their Ledgers in order. Cutover comes last, once the new quality is accepted: register the new artifacts, retire the old producers and regenerate every Session through the app.

Initial definitions of scene, beat and thread were agreed on 2026-10-29, with IDs required on every scene, beat, thread and fact; the ID scheme itself is the agent's recommendation. Pass 1 of the first experiment, a [hand run of steps 1 to 3 on Session 004](experiments/session-004/README.md), is done. It supported the definitions, and the user decided its findings the same day; they are recorded as the intent's Facts definitions.

Pass 2 is done: [prompts for steps 1 to 3, run through Opus 5.5 on Session 004](experiments/session-004/pass-2/README.md). Steps 1 and 2 met the quality bar. Step 3 as designed, with plain parallel per-thread calls, did not reliably give each fact one home. Adding a homes step, which names one home thread per shared subject before the parallel calls, met the whole bar in three of three final runs. A single-call variant did better than plain parallel calls but still repeated details. Facts in every variant sometimes overreach their beats.

On 2026-10-29 the user chose the homes step for step 3: step 2 names one home thread per shared subject, and step 3 stays parallel. The homes step is now folded into the step 2 prompt, and the final prompts met the quality bar on both Session 004 chains. On Session 003 they transferred well, apart from three gaps: a missed Mystery, a missed obstacle link, and the shirt on two threads. Each was fixed with one rule. The final prompts met the quality bar on Session 003 and on both Session 004 chains. A last faithfulness round removed most speculative questions and invented links. Prompt iteration then stopped: each further rule traded one judgement slip for another (an inferred possibly moot flag, over-marked uncertainty), which steps 5 and 9 are designed to catch.

# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map whose marked places they could not recognize.
- Reginald the frog warned Dr. Uriah that a trickster Clurichen comes from the riverbanks into town each night.
- Dunk drank with Farmer Gill, who told how Sir Brandon killed the town's first dragon centuries ago.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty for the dragon; Sir Phidipaldi then slapped him and challenged him to a duel.
- Drop Dead Ned, a thief with a killer wasp, offered Dr. Uriah his services for hire.
- Sir Phidipaldi agreed to help Quinn, who runs the Golden Egg, find out who empties his ale each night.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure shown on the party's map.
- Kill the black dragon and drag its body into town to collect Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Catch whoever empties Quinn's good ale at the Golden Egg each night and collect his 100 gold.

## What could help
- Lady Hilda, a seasoned fighter, says that if the party pays her, she will join their dragon hunt. She won't go alone.
- If Dr. Uriah hires Drop Dead Ned for 100 gold a week, Ned and his killer wasp will do any job. Ned insists on killing people with the wasp.
- If Sir Phidipaldi makes a show of delivering fake ale to the Golden Egg, they may see who comes for it.

## Still unanswered
- Where are the places marked on the treasure map, and which holds the treasure?
- Who or what empties Quinn's good ale each night?
- Where does the black dragon lurk outside Brandonsford?
- What happened to Bentley's overdue shipment, and why has nothing reached Brandonsford in months?
- Where did this new dragon come from, centuries after Sir Brandon's was killed?

## Meanwhile
- The black dragon tore off the woodsman George's arm and may maim or kill others, including Gill's three sons now hunting it.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is with Quinn in the empty Golden Egg Tavern. Wayman and Dr. Uriah are across the street in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm on the edge of town, staying the night in the barn.
- Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn are about to stage a fake ale shipment. Quinn will bring bottles and they will fill them with water. Then Sir Phidipaldi will make a big show of delivering them to see what happens. Quinn offered 100 gold for the help.

# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: "I like to watch him sleep" - Ingrid**

- **Your treasure map.** Father William, the priest, marked the Barrow Mound and the witch's home on it. You still don't know where all its marked places are, or which one holds the treasure.
- **The Brandonsford dragon.** Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold for its body, and Gill's three sons are hunting it now. George, who survived its lair below the northern mountains, says nothing pierces its scales.
- **The Clurichen on the riverbanks.** Trout bagged Naggeneen, the ale thief, and Quinn hung him behind the bar and paid his captors.
- **Bentley's missing shipment.** It is still overdue, and you don't know why nothing has reached Brandonsford in months.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** For a hundred gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do any job, as long as Ned gets to kill people.
- **Squints and the Halfling Corps.** Trout recruited the halfling, then sent him in as expendable bait. In return, Trout owes him second pick of the first treasure, after the elite members.

- **Brandon's buried sword.** Father William says it lies in the Barrow Mound and is the only weapon that can kill the dragon. George says no one knows if that's true or where it is.
- **Dr. Uriah's debt to Father William.** In return for help with the map, he owes penance at the Barrow Mound and ten gold for its upkeep.
- **The giant in the stone cottage.** Squints freed Brother Dirk from its sack while it slept, and you outran it across a field.
- **Brother Dirk.** Trout has assigned him to report to Squints.
- **The faun grove.** Dunk out-drank the fauns there and traded a rabbit's foot for a flask of their wine.
- **Ingrid's news about fairies.** She told Dr. Uriah that long-hidden fairies are coming out more often.

## Where you are

You stand together in the woods north of town, across the field from the giant's stone cottage, with Squints and Brother Dirk beside you. You're bound for the Barrow and pressing Dirk to come along, but he says he should just go home and hasn't agreed.

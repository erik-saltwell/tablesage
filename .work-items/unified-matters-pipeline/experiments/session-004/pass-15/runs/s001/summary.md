# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map to places they did not recognize.
- Reginald the frog warned Dr. Uriah that a trickster Clurichen comes from the riverbanks into town nightly.
- Dunk drank the night away with Farmer Gill, who told how Sir Brandon slew the town's first dragon.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty for killing the dragon. Sir Phidipaldi then slapped him and challenged him to a duel.
- Drop Dead Ned, a thief with a killer wasp, offered to hire on with Dr. Uriah.
- Sir Phidipaldi agreed to help Quinn, who runs the Golden Egg, catch whoever empties his ale.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure shown on the party's map.
- Kill the black dragon and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold. The party never formally accepted.
- Catch whoever empties Quinn's good ale at the Golden Egg each night and collect his 100 gold.

## What could help

- If Dr. Uriah pays Drop Dead Ned 100 gold a week, Ned and his killer wasp will do any job. Ned insists on killing people.
- If Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn stage the fake ale delivery, they can watch who comes for the ale.

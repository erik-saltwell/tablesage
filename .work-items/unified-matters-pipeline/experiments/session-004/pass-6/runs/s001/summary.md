# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map to places they did not recognize.
- Reginald the frog warned the party to avoid a Clurichen trickster that comes into town every night.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty on a black dragon that took a woodsman's arm. Sir Phidipaldi then slapped Eric with his glove.
- Lady Hilda offered to hire on with the party, and Sir Phidipaldi said they would return after seeing the Reeve.
- Drop Dead Ned offered himself and his killer wasp for hire, and Dr. Uriah said he would think about it.
- Bentley and Farmer Gill told the party that supplies have stopped reaching Brandonsford.
- Quinn promised Sir Phidipaldi payment for helping him get back at rival tavern keeper Bentley.

## Your goals
- Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect his promised 100 gold.
- Find the treasure on the party's treasure map.
- Kill the black dragon outside Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Buy Farmer Gill's mule Shamus for 100 gold.

## What could help
- If Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn stage a showy fake ale delivery at the Golden Egg, they could see who comes for the stock.
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join them against the dragon.
- The party has a treasure map marking several unfamiliar locations around Brandonsford.
- If Dunk kills the dragon outside town, Farmer Gill might trade him Shamus. Gill never actually agreed to this.

## Meanwhile

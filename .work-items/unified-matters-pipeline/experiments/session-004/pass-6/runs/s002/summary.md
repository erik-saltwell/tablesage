# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: "Ow, why did you do that?" - Squints**

## Last session...
- Trout recruited the halfling Squints as a junior assistant in his Halfling Corps. Trout later assigned rescued Brother Dirk to report to Squints.
- Dunk spotted a glowing mithril chain shirt in Warwick the smith's shop. He discovered that Ingrid the alchemist secretly watches Warwick sleep.
- The party caught the invisible booze thief, a Clurichen named Naggeneen, and handed him over to Quinn.
- Dunk agreed to help Ingrid with a sleeping potion for Warwick and gave her watered-down faun wine from the grove.
- George told the party about the dragon and doubted Eric the Reeve can pay the bounty. Sir Phidipaldi vowed to bring his lance.
- The party set out through the forest for Sir Brandon's Barrow Mound, telling Dunk to meet them there.
- Dr. Uriah bargained with Father William, who marked the Barrow Mound and the witch's house on the party's map.
- Squints pulled Brother Dirk from a sleeping giant's cottage, and the party outran the waking giant.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure from the party's treasure map.
- Kill the black dragon outside Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Get Farmer Gill's mule Shamus.

## Standing in your way
- George says arrows and swords can't pierce the dragon's black scales. Its poisoned bite kills, and it spits sulfur.
- Farmer Gill won't sell Shamus for less than 100 gold until his sons return from hunting the dragon. Without them or a mule, he can't work.

## What could help
- Dr. Uriah carries the party's treasure map, which marks several unrecognized locations around Brandonsford.
- Father William says that if the party retrieves the magic sword buried with Sir Brandon in the Barrow Mound, they can kill the dragon.
- George says the dragon's lair is north at the foot of the mountains, with no climbing needed.
- If the party checks the map location they planned to visit on the way to the Barrow, they may find what it marks.
- Squints has joined the party as Trout's junior assistant and takes Trout's orders.
- Dunk says he has a plan to get Warwick's mithril chain shirt, and that it could help against the dragon.
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join them in going after the dragon.

## Meanwhile

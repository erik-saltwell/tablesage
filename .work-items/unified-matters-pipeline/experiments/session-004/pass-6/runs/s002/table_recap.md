# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: "Ow, why did you do that?" - Squints**

- **The treasure map.** You're still after its treasure. Dr. Uriah carries it, and it marks several unrecognized places around Brandonsford. You planned to check one on the way to the Barrow.
- **The invisible booze thief.** You caught him, a Clurichen named Naggeneen, and handed him over to Quinn.
- **The dragon.** Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold for its body, though George doubts he can pay. George says arrows and swords can't pierce its scales, and it can't fly yet but soon will.
- **Lady Hilda's offer.** She will join you against the dragon if you pay her. Sir Phidipaldi told her you'd come back for her after talking with Eric the Reeve.
- **Brandonsford's missing shipment.** You still don't know what is stopping goods from reaching town.
- **Farmer Gill's mule Shamus.** Farmer Gill won't sell him for less than a hundred gold until his sons return from hunting the dragon. Without them or the mule, he can't work.
- **Warwick's mithril chain shirt.** Dunk spotted it glowing in Warwick the smith's shop, and says he has a plan to get it. He says it could help against the dragon.
- **The Halfling Corps.** Trout recruited the halfling Squints as his junior assistant, and Brother Dirk the monk now reports to Squints. Trout owes him second pick of your first treasure, after the elite members.
- **Ingrid's sleeping potion.** Dunk learned Ingrid the alchemist secretly watches Warwick sleep. He gave her watered-down faun wine for it. Once it's made, he must help her into Warwick's house to give it to him.
- **Sir Brandon's magic sword.** Father William says it's buried with Sir Brandon in the Barrow Mound, and that with it you can kill the dragon. Dunk is to meet you there.
- **Vivian, the witch in the woods.** It's still unclear whether she and Ingrid are a coven or just friends.
- **The bargain with Father William.** He marked the Barrow Mound and the witch's house on your map. In return, Dr. Uriah owes him penance at the Mound.
- **Fairies coming out of hiding.** Long-hidden fairies are appearing more often, and you still don't know why.
- **The giant's cottage.** Squints pulled Brother Dirk out while the giant slept, and you outran it when it woke.

## Where you are

You're in the woods heading north toward the Barrow, just past the field around the giant's stone cottage. Squints and Brother Dirk are with you.

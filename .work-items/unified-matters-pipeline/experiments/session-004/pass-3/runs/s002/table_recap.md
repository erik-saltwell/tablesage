# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: The invisible Clurichen slipping on Sir Phidipaldi's marbles**

- **Your treasure map.** Father William marked the witch's home on Dr. Uriah's map, and you planned a forest route around it. You assume the treasure lies at one of the map's marked spots, but the land around Brandonsford is wholly unknown to you. One of those spots is on your forest route to the Barrow Mound, and you plan to check it on the way.
- **The black dragon near Brandonsford.** George told you how it killed all his friends, and Sir Phidipaldi vowed to bring his lance against it. George says one poisoned bite kills, nothing pierces its black scales, and its lair is up north at the foot of the mountains, with no climbing needed. He also says it can't fly yet but soon will, and then they're all doomed.
- **Lady Hilda at the Clumsy Fox.** She will join you against the dragon for pay, though she says she couldn't kill it herself.
- **Sir Phidipaldi's challenge.** He slapped Eric the Reeve with his glove and demanded a duel at dawn, but Eric hasn't accepted.
- **Warwick the smith's mithril shirt.** Dunk spotted the glowing, magical chain shirt through the shop window and told you he has a plan to get it. He says it could help against the dragon. No one knows why Warwick always refuses to sell it.
- **Trout's Halfling Corps.** Trout recruited Squints as a junior assistant, then gave him Brother Dirk as a follower. Squints fights with sling and dagger, is more experienced than Trout, and takes Trout's orders against monsters. Trout owes him second pick of treasure, after the elite members, once the first treasure is won.
- **Dunk's sleeping-potion deal with Ingrid.** He agreed to help Ingrid into Warwick's house and give Warwick her sleeping potion, but they haven't settled whether to meet tonight or tomorrow. He secretly watered the faun wine he gave her down to one part in five, and no one knows whether the potion will still work. Ingrid climbs every night to Warwick's second-story bedroom window, which is cracked open.
- **Sir Brandon's magic sword.** You set out for the Barrow Mound to retrieve it, though Brother Dirk wanted to go home instead. Father William says only this sword can kill the dragon, but George says no one knows the truth of the legend that it lies in the mound. Father William called the mound a day's walk, while you reckon it's about three miles.
- **Dr. Uriah's pledge.** After Father William measured his feet and found no webbing, Uriah promised to perform penance at the Barrow Mound and donate ten gold for its upkeep.
- **The man-eating giant.** You rescued Brother Dirk from its sack and outran it across the field. It is slow, but it may strike again if you pass its cottage.

## Where you are

You stand in the field beyond the giant's cottage, in the woods north of Brandonsford, with Squints and Brother Dirk beside you.

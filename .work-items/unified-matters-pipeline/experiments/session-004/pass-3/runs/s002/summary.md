# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: The invisible Clurichen slipping on Sir Phidipaldi's marbles**

## Last session...
- Father William marked the witch's home on Dr. Uriah's map, and the party planned a forest route around it.
- Trout recruited Squints as a junior assistant in the Halfling Corps, then gave him Brother Dirk as a follower.
- The party caught Naggeneen the Clurichen stealing Quinn's ale, and Quinn paid each of them 33 gold.
- Dunk spotted Warwick's glowing mithril shirt through the shop window and told the party he has a plan to get it.
- Dr. Uriah tracked the invisible Clurichen from the river, and it now hangs in a bag behind Quinn's bar.
- Dunk agreed to help Ingrid with a sleeping potion, then gave her faun wine he had secretly watered down.
- George told the party how the dragon killed his friends, and Sir Phidipaldi vowed to bring his lance against it.
- The party set out for the Barrow Mound, but Brother Dirk wanted to go home rather than join them.
- Dr. Uriah pledged penance to Father William after the priest measured his feet and found no webbing.
- The party rescued Brother Dirk from a man-eating giant's sack and outran the giant across the field.

## Your goals
- Retrieve Sir Brandon's magic sword from the Barrow Mound. Father William says it is needed to kill the dragon.
- Find the treasure on the party's map, which the party assumes lies at one of its marked spots.
- Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford.
- Get Warwick's magical mithril chain shirt, which Dunk says could help against the dragon.
- Fight Eric the Reeve in the dawn duel Sir Phidipaldi demanded by slapping him with his glove.
- Buy Farmer Gill's mule Shamus for less. Dunk has offered 20 gold.

## Standing in your way
- George says nothing pierces the dragon's black scales. Father William says only Brandon's magic sword can kill it.
- Eric the Reeve hasn't accepted Sir Phidipaldi's challenge, so the dawn duel can't happen yet.
- Farmer Gill won't lower Shamus's price until his sons return from hunting the dragon. He can't work without them or the mule.

## What could help
- George says the dragon's lair is up north at the foot of the mountains, with no climbing needed.
- One of the map's marked spots lies on the forest route to the Barrow Mound. The party plans to check it on the way.
- Lady Hilda at the Clumsy Fox will join the party against the dragon for pay. She says she couldn't kill it herself.
- Ingrid's shop sells healing mandrake root tea for 10 gold, witch powder that glows on magic for 7, and wolfsbane against wolves for 5.
- Squints is a sling and dagger fighter more experienced than Trout. He fights monsters for the Halfling Corps under Trout's orders.
- Warwick's second-story bedroom window is cracked open, offering a way into his house. Ingrid climbs up to it every night.
- Father William marked the Barrow Mound on Dr. Uriah's map. He called it a day's walk, but the party reckons about three miles.

## Still unanswered
- Where are the places marked on the party's map? The land around Brandonsford is wholly unknown to the party.
- Why does Warwick always refuse to sell his glowing, magical mithril shirt?
- Is Brandon's magic sword really in the Barrow Mound? George says no one knows the truth of the legend.
- Will Ingrid's sleeping potion still work with the faun wine Dunk watered down to one part in five?
- Where did this new dragon come from, since Sir Brandon's dragon was killed centuries ago?

## Promises and debts
- Dunk must help Ingrid into Warwick's house and give Warwick her sleeping potion. They haven't settled whether to meet tonight or tomorrow.
- Dr. Uriah must perform penance at the Barrow Mound and donate 10 gold for its upkeep, as promised to Father William.
- Trout owes Squints second pick of treasure, after the elite members, once the first treasure is won.

## Meanwhile
- The black dragon near Brandonsford may harm others. George says one poisoned bite kills, and it killed all his friends.
- George says the dragon can't fly yet but soon will, and then they're all doomed.
- The man-eating giant in the north woods may strike again if the party passes its cottage. It is slow and can be outrun.

## Where you are
- The party is in the field beyond the giant's cottage in the woods north of Brandonsford, with Squints and the newly rescued Brother Dirk.

# Brandonsford

## Last session...
- The party hired Drop Dead Ned for the Barrow Mound, and Ned offered Wayman 5 gold per goblin body.
- The party opened Sir Brandon's tomb, and Sir Phidipaldi took the dragon slayer's sword and armor.
- Sir Phidipaldi planned to carry out Dunk's scheme for the mithril chain shirt once the sword was won.

- Goblins and a talking slime warned the party of a mad undead giant who cut down goblins.

## Your goals
- Kill the dragon near Brandonsford for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Obtain protection against the black dragon's poison before going after the dragon.
- Find the treasure marked on the party's map.

## Standing in your way
- The undead giant swordsman who killed many goblins stands in the way of clearing the evil from the Barrow Mound.
- George says nothing pierces the black dragon's scales. Arrows and swords have all failed against them.
- The Free Goblin Society would not want their dead defiled. Giving the goblin bodies to Ned means sneaking them past the goblins.

## What could help
- Sir Phidipaldi holds Sir Brandon's magical, uncursed sword, etched "All shadows yield to my light". Father William says only this sword can kill the dragon.
- The party holds a jeweled ceremonial dagger, seven 50-gold candleholders, a 20-gold gold-toothed skull and five 100-gold urns. Dr. Uriah values the dagger at over 200 gold in a major city.
- Drop Dead Ned, the inn's killer with a little killer wasp, scouts and kills for the party in the Barrow Mound for a 100 gold fee.

## Meanwhile

# Brandonsford
**2026-09-01: Four eight-foot knight statues labeled Valor Piety Wisdom Duty**

- **The dragon near Brandonsford.** Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold bounty for killing it. George says nothing pierces the black dragon's scales, and that arrows and swords have all failed, and he warns it will soon fly and doom Brandonsford. Where this new dragon came from is still unknown.
- **Sir Brandon's sword.** You opened Sir Brandon's tomb, and Sir Phidipaldi took the dragon slayer's sword and armor. The sword is magical and uncursed, etched with the words "All shadows yield to my light." Father William says only this sword can kill the dragon.
- **Dunk's deal.** Sir Phidipaldi planned to carry out Dunk's scheme for the mithril chain shirt once the sword was won. Under it, Dunk must help Ingrid get into Warwick's house and give Warwick the sleeping potion.
- **Dr. Uriah's penance.** He still owes Father William penance at the Barrow Mound. He also owes a ten gold donation for the Mound's upkeep.
- **The Barrow Mound treasure.** You hold a jeweled ceremonial dagger, seven candleholders worth fifty gold each, a gold-toothed skull worth twenty, and five urns worth a hundred each. Dr. Uriah values the dagger at over two hundred gold in a major city. You must let Squints pick second, after the elite members.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** You hired him, the inn's killer with a little killer wasp, to scout and kill for you in the Barrow Mound for a hundred gold fee. He offered Wayman five gold per goblin body. The Free Goblin Society would not want their dead defiled, so handing bodies to Ned means sneaking them past the goblins.
- **Protection from the dragon's poison.** You aim to get it before going after the dragon. Sir Phidipaldi's plan calls for getting it from the witch or the alchemist.
- **The Free Goblin Society.** Posing as Brandon's son, Sir Phidipaldi struck a bargain with them. You must clear the evil out of the Barrow Mound, and in return the goblins live there and let you take Sir Brandon's goods. The undead giant swordsman stands in the way of that.
- **The undead giant swordsman.** It is still unknown whether he is Alfred, Sir Brandon's shaggy companion shown with a greatsword in the mosaics.
- **The robes of Saint Arthur.** Dr. Uriah judged them uncursed. He needs Ingrid's witch powder to test whether they have magical properties.
- **The jeweled chalice.** Brother Dirk says it was surely a gift to the church. Whether it belongs to the church, and whether you will give it up, is still open.

## Where you are

You stand inside Sir Brandon's tomb, ready to leave. In the doorway looms a seven-foot undead brown skeleton in rusted armor and a mustachioed death mask, dragging a huge greatsword. The goblins say it killed many of their friends. The slime says it is very mad.

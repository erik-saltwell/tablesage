# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Drop Dead Ned's killer wasp buzzing in a necklace jar**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map into land they didn't know.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty on a dragon troubling Brandonsford, the town's first since Sir Brandon's legendary kill.
- Lady Hilda, a seasoned fighter at the Clumsy Fox, offered to join the party against the dragon for pay.
- Sir Phidipaldi dismissed George's black dragon as an illusion and slapped Eric the Reeve with his glove.
- Dr. Uriah met the thief Drop Dead Ned, who offered himself and his killer wasp for hire.
- Sir Phidipaldi struck a deal with Quinn of the Golden Egg to get back at Bentley over stolen ale.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure on the party's map, which the party assumes lies at one of its marked spots.
- Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford, though the party hasn't formally taken the job.
- Fight Eric the Reeve in the dawn duel Sir Phidipaldi demanded.

## Standing in your way
- Eric the Reeve hasn't accepted Sir Phidipaldi's challenge, so the dawn duel can't go ahead.

## What could help
- The one-armed woodsman George met the dragon and calls it a black dragon, not a big one. Eric says George lives a couple of houses down from the Clumsy Fox and might come by tonight.
- Quinn and Sir Phidipaldi agreed to stage a fake ale shipment at the Golden Egg to see what happens overnight. Quinn brings bottles to fill with water, and Sir Phidipaldi makes a show of delivering them.
- Eric the Reeve offers 1000 gold to whoever kills the dragon and drags its body into Brandonsford.
- Lady Hilda could help the party fight the dragon if they pay her.
- Sir Phidipaldi told Lady Hilda they would come back for her after talking to the Reeve. She says she couldn't kill the dragon herself.
- Drop Dead Ned offers himself and his killer wasp for 100 gold a week, if he gets to kill people with it. He'll rebate 5 gold per kill if he can do what he wants with the victims.
- Farmer Gill offers to sell Dunk his shabby mule Shamus for 100 gold.

## Still unanswered
- Who or what really takes Quinn's good stock each night, leaving glasses empty or knocked over? Quinn blames Bentley.
- Where are the places marked on the party's treasure map, in land the party doesn't know?
- Where is the dragon to be found outside Brandonsford?
- What has stopped shipments reaching Brandonsford for months, and what happened to Bentley's restock due a week ago?
- Why won't Warwick the smith sell his mithril chain shirt? Gill says it may be the best in the world and fits a normal-sized person.

## Promises and debts
- Sir Phidipaldi must help Quinn get back at Bentley, and Quinn will pay him 100 gold for it.

## Meanwhile
- Farmer Gill's three sons are hunting the dragon now. They may kill it and claim the Reeve's bounty first.
- Reginald the Frog warns that a trickster Clurichen comes into town from the riverbanks each night. He says to stay away from wherever it goes.
- A black dragon near Brandonsford tore off George's arm and may harm others.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is in the Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Bentley escorted him over from the Clumsy Fox Tavern across the street.

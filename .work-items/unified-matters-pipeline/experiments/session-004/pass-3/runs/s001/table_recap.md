# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Drop Dead Ned's killer wasp buzzing in a necklace jar**

- **The treasure map.** It led you to Brandonsford at sunset, into land you don't know. You still don't know where its marked places are, and you're assuming the treasure lies at one of them.
- **The Clurichen.** Reginald the Frog warns that this trickster comes into town from the riverbanks each night. He says to stay away from wherever it goes.
- **The dragon.** Eric the Reeve offers one thousand gold to whoever kills it and drags its body into Brandonsford. It's the town's first bounty since Sir Brandon's legendary kill. You haven't formally taken the job, and you don't know where outside town the dragon is found. George, a one-armed woodsman, met it and calls it a black dragon, not a big one. It tore off his arm and may harm others. Eric says George lives a couple of houses down from the Clumsy Fox and might come by tonight. Farmer Gill's three sons are hunting the dragon now and may claim the bounty first.
- **Lady Hilda.** She's a seasoned fighter at the Clumsy Fox who offered to join you against the dragon for pay. She says she couldn't kill it herself. Sir Phidipaldi told her you'd come back for her after talking to the Reeve.
- **Bentley's missing shipment.** No shipments have reached Brandonsford for months, and you don't know what's stopping them. Bentley's own restock was due a week ago, and what happened to it is still a mystery.
- **Sir Phidipaldi's challenge.** He dismissed George's black dragon as an illusion and slapped Eric the Reeve with his glove, demanding a duel at dawn. Eric hasn't accepted, so the duel can't go ahead.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** Dr. Uriah met this thief, who offers himself and his killer wasp for a hundred gold a week, as long as he gets to kill people with it. He'll give back five gold per kill if he can do what he wants with the victims.
- **Shamus the mule.** Farmer Gill offers to sell this shabby animal to Dunk for a hundred gold.
- **Warwick's mithril shirt.** Warwick the smith won't sell it, and nobody knows why. Gill says it may be the best in the world, and it fits a normal-sized person.
- **Quinn's vanishing ale.** Every night something takes the good stock at the Golden Egg, leaving glasses empty or knocked over. Quinn, the owner, blames Bentley. Sir Phidipaldi must help Quinn get back at Bentley, and Quinn will pay him a hundred gold for it. They've agreed to stage a fake ale shipment to see what happens overnight. Quinn brings bottles to fill with water, and Sir Phidipaldi makes a show of delivering them.

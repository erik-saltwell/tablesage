- Drop Dead Ned's blood-red wasp tore Sir Alfred apart, and the party left the undead knight helpless but not destroyed.
- The party brought Sir Brandon's sword out of the barrow, and Vivian advised drugging the dragon with faun wine.
- The goblins agreed to settle only the upper Barrow Mound and wall off the stairs. Wayman promised never to tell King Hogboon where they are.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure marked on the party's map.
- Kill the dragon near Brandonsford for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Obtain protection or a remedy against the dragon's poison before facing the dragon.

## Standing in your way
- A cave-in seals the Dwarven mine, blocking the metal owed to Warwick. It can be dug through, and Dunk has partly broken in.
- George says nothing pierces the black dragon's scales. Arrows and swords have all failed against them.

## What could help
- Grimny's ghost said Bral probably hid the dwarves' hoard in the Dragon's Lair marked on the party's map.
- The party holds Sir Brandon's sword, a magic sword especially effective against evil beings.
- The party's map marks the Dragon's Lair. Grimny says the dwarves found that cave empty, unvisited and very small, just one big room.
- Dunk has enough faun wine for the hunt. Vivian says enough of it would knock the dragon out so the sword could be dragged through its mouth as Sir Brandon did.
- Sir Phidipaldi plans to dose caught game with faun wine and leave it outside the dragon's cave. Once the dragon eats it, the party goes in.
- Wayman wears Warwick's mithril shirt on loan, greatly improving his protection and halving damage from magic weapons and spells.
- Cedric's signed note promises three-quarters price, about 975 gold pieces, for the barrow treasure once caravans flow and his messenger can truck the goods to town. The party may no longer hold it.
- Dr. Uriah's Read Languages spell can read the writing under one map location per day.

- Where did the new dragon near Brandonsford come from?
- What did Sir Brandon really do against the dragon? Vivian says only Sir Brandon told the tale, and the church will not let her near it.

# Brandonsford
**2026-09-22: "My loins will forever burn with the heat of the forge" - Vivian**

- **The treasure map.** Dr. Uriah's new spell identified two of its marked places, the Dragon's Lair and the Dwarven mine. His Read Languages spell can read the writing under one location per day, and you are still after the treasure the map marks.
- **Eric the Reeve's bounty.** He offers a thousand gold to whoever kills the black dragon near Brandonsford. George says nothing pierces its scales, since arrows and swords have all failed, and he warns it will soon fly and doom the town. Where this new dragon came from is still unknown.
- **Your plan against the dragon.** You brought Sir Brandon's sword out of the barrow, a magic blade especially effective against evil beings. Vivian advises knocking the dragon out with faun wine so the sword can be dragged through its mouth, as Sir Brandon did, and Dunk has enough wine. Sir Phidipaldi plans to dose caught game, leave it outside the cave, and go in once the dragon eats it.
- **The Dragon's Lair.** The ghost of the dwarf Grimny says Bral probably hid the dwarves' hoard there, and that the dwarves found the cave empty, unvisited and small, just one big room. Dunk suspects Bral only pretends a dragon lives there to keep everyone away.
- **The dragon's poison.** You still want protection or a remedy against it before facing the dragon.
- **Sir Alfred, the undead knight.** Drop Dead Ned's blood-red wasp tore him apart, and you left him helpless but not destroyed in the Barrow Mound. He may recover in Sir Brandon's tomb and attack you if you return, or kill the goblins settling the upper mound unless their wall across the stairs holds him.
- **Cedric's promissory note.** He signed it for the barrow treasure, promising about nine hundred seventy-five gold once caravans flow. You used it to hire Malzazarik and Lady Hilda, so you may no longer hold it, and you owe them payment from it.
- **Your deal with Warwick.** He lent you his grandfather's mithril shirt, which Wayman now wears, and it halves damage from magic weapons and spells. In return you owe him metal from the Dwarven mine and the shirt's return, while he keeps your plate armor and Sir Phidipaldi's lyre. A cave-in seals the mine, but it can be dug through, and Dunk has partly broken in.
- **The dead dwarf's ring.** Dunk pulled this magic ring from a dead dwarf's hand in the cave-in and is wearing it. No one knows if it is cursed.
- **Who killed Grimny.** His ghost, unaware he had died, believes Bral, the greediest of the seven brothers, killed him in the mine cave-in. Bral is the dwarf Sir Phidipaldi and Dr. Uriah won the map from in a bet, and where he is now is unknown.

## Where you are

You stand at the dug-out entrance of the Dwarven mine, beside the burnt cabin at the foot of the hill. Grimny's ghost has just vanished.

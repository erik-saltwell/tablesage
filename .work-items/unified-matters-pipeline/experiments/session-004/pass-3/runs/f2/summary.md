# Brandonsford
**2026-09-22: Drop Dead Ned's baseball-sized blood-red wasp inside Sir Alfred's ribs**

## Last session...
- Drop Dead Ned's blood-red wasp made Sir Alfred tear himself apart, and the party left him undestroyed.
- The party took Sir Brandon's magic sword from the barrow, and Vivian advised knocking the dragon out with faun wine.
- Wayman persuaded four goblins to settle the Barrow Mound's upper rooms and promised to hide them from King Hogboon.
- Dr. Uriah promised Brother Dirk to return the barrow robes to the church after the dragon is defeated.
- Dr. Uriah began reading the map's labels, revealing the Dragon's Lair and the Dwarven mine.
- Cedric signed a promissory note for the barrow treasure, and the party used it to recruit Malzazarik and Lady Hilda.
- Warwick lent the party his mithril shirt and took Sir Phidipaldi's lyre and the plate armor as collateral.
- Ingrid led the party to Vivian, the witch of the wood, who had nothing against the dragon's venom.
- Grimny's ghost told Dunk which of his six brothers he believes killed him.
- Dunk's ring let him speak with Grimny's ghost, who ascended after reading Vivian's letter.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure marked on the party's map.
- Kill the dragon near Brandonsford for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Get protection against the poison George the Hunter reported, before facing the dragon.

## Standing in your way
- A cave-in seals the Dwarven mine's entrance, blocking the party from getting Warwick's metal. It looks natural and can be dug through.
- George says nothing pierces the black dragon's scales. Arrows and swords have all failed against them.
- Cedric cannot pay the promissory note until the caravans flow again.

## What could help
- The party holds Sir Brandon's magic sword, which is especially effective against evil beings.
- The party already has enough faun wine. Vivian says it could knock the dragon out so they can drag Sir Brandon's sword through its mouth, if they can reach it.
- The wizard Malzazarik and Lady Hilda have joined the party against the dragon.
- The party's map marks the Dragon's Lair. Grimny says it is a very small one-room cave the dwarves scouted several times and found empty.
- Grimny says his brother Bral probably hid the dwarves' mine hoard in the Dragon's Lair. The party believes Bral is the dwarf who lost them the map in a bet.
- Cedric's promissory note will pay the party 975 gold, three-quarters price for the barrow treasure, once the caravans flow again. The party has offered it to pay its new hirelings.
- Sir Phidipaldi proposes leaving faun-wine-dosed game outside the dragon's cave, then going in once the dragon has eaten it.
- Wayman wears Warwick's mithril chain shirt on loan. It greatly improves his protection and halves damage from magic weapons and spells.

## Still unanswered
- Is there really a dragon, or is Bral only pretending there is one to keep everybody away, as Dunk suspects?
- Grimny's ghost believes Bral, the greediest of the seven dwarf brothers, killed him. Did Bral really kill Grimny, and how?
- Vivian says only Sir Brandon told his tale, and the church knows it. What did he really do against the dragon?
- What became of Grimny's other brothers, Grelli, Durney, Brenny, Tedry, Rooney and Bral?
- Dunk felt a growing urge to wear the dead dwarf's magic ring, and Dr. Uriah's witch powder couldn't tell. Is the ring cursed?

## Promises and debts

- The party must return Warwick's mithril chain shirt, lent for the mine trip. Ingrid says it was his grandfather's armor.
- The party owes Malzazarik and Lady Hilda payment for joining, offered out of Cedric's promissory note.
- Dr. Uriah must do penance at the Barrow Mound for Father William.
- Dr. Uriah must return the barrow robes to Brother Dirk's church once the dragon is defeated. The robes are not magical, just a religious relic.

## Meanwhile

- With Wayman's encouragement, the goblins settling the Barrow Mound's upper rooms have declared destroying its church their first job as a free state.
- Sir Alfred lies broken apart but not destroyed in the Barrow Mound's lower level. He may rise again and kill anyone in the mound, including the Free Goblin Society members staying there.

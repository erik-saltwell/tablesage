# Brandonsford
**2026-09-22: Drop Dead Ned's baseball-sized blood-red wasp inside Sir Alfred's ribs**

- **The treasure map.** Dr. Uriah has begun reading its labels, which include the Dragon's Lair and the Dwarven mine. Grimny, the murdered dwarf, says his brother Bral probably hid the dwarves' mine hoard in the Dragon's Lair. You believe Bral is the dwarf who lost you the map in a bet.
- **Eric the Reeve's dragon bounty.** He offers one thousand gold to kill the black dragon near Brandonsford, and George the Hunter says it will soon fly and doom the town, and that arrows and swords have all failed against its scales. You now carry Sir Brandon's magic sword from the barrow, which is especially effective against evil beings, and you have enough faun wine, which Vivian, the witch of the wood, says could knock the dragon out so you can drag the sword through its mouth, if you can reach it. Grimny describes the Lair as a very small one-room cave the dwarves scouted several times and found empty, and Dunk suspects Bral is only pretending there's a dragon to keep everybody away.
- **The goblins in the Barrow Mound.** Wayman persuaded four of them to settle its upper rooms and promised to hide them from King Hogboon. With his encouragement, they've declared destroying the mound's church their first job as a free state.
- **Sir Alfred, the undead knight.** Drop Dead Ned's blood-red wasp made him tear himself apart, but you left him broken and undestroyed in the mound's lower level. He may rise again and kill anyone in the mound, including the Free Goblin Society members staying there.
- **Your new allies and Cedric's note.** The wizard Malzazarik and Lady Hilda have joined you against the dragon, and you owe them payment, offered out of Cedric's promissory note. Cedric signed it for the barrow treasure, and it will pay you nine hundred seventy-five gold, three-quarters price. He can't pay it until the caravans flow again.
- **Warwick's deal.** You must bring him metal from the Dwarven mine, and until then he holds Sir Phidipaldi's lyre and your plate armor as collateral. Wayman wears Warwick's mithril chain shirt on loan, and it greatly improves his protection and halves damage from magic weapons and spells. You must return it, and Ingrid says it was Warwick's grandfather's armor.
- **Who killed Grimny.** His ghost told Dunk he believes it was Bral, the greediest of the seven dwarf brothers. Whether Bral really did it, and how, is still unknown. So is what became of the other brothers.
- **Grimny's ring.** Dunk used it to speak with Grimny's ghost, who ascended after reading Vivian's letter. Dunk feels a growing urge to wear it, and Dr. Uriah's witch powder couldn't tell whether it is cursed.

## Where you are

You stand at the caved-in entrance of the Dwarven mine, beside the burnt cabin. The cave-in looks natural, and it can be dug through.

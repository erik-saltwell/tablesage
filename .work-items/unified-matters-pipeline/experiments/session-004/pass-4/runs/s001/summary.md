# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map to places they did not recognize.
- Reginald the frog warned the party to avoid the Clurichen, a trickster that roams town each night.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty on the black dragon that took woodsman George's arm. Sir Phidipaldi then insulted Eric and slapped him with a glove.
- Lady Hilda offered to join the party on the dragon hunt for pay.
- The thief Drop Dead Ned offered himself and his killer wasp for hire at 100 gold a week.
- Bentley and Farmer Gill told the party that supplies have stopped reaching Brandonsford.
- Sir Phidipaldi struck a deal with Quinn to get back at Bentley, whom Quinn blames for his vanishing ale.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure shown on the party's treasure map.
- The party is considering killing the black dragon and dragging its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Dunk is considering acquiring Farmer Gill's prize mule, Shamus. Gill says it would let Dunk start his own farm.

## What could help
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join them on the dragon hunt.
- Lady Hilda has chain mail and a shield but says she cannot kill the dragon herself. She has named no fee.
- If Dunk pays Farmer Gill 100 gold, Gill will sell him Shamus.
- If Dunk helps kill the dragon, Gill might give him Shamus, though Gill never agreed to that trade.

## Still unanswered
- What empties or knocks over Quinn's stock every night?
- What is the Clurichen doing in Brandonsford each night?
- Why have no goods reached Brandonsford in months?

## Promises and debts
- Quinn will pay Sir Phidipaldi 100 gold for helping him get back at Bentley.
- Sir Phidipaldi must stage a showy fake ale delivery of water-filled bottles at the Golden Egg with Quinn.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is in the empty Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm, drinking with Gill on the porch and planning to sleep in the barn.

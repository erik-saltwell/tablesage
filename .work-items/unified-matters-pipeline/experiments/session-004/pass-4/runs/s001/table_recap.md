# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

- **The treasure map.** It led you to Brandonsford, where you arrived at sunset, though the places it shows are ones you don't recognize. The treasure itself is still to be found.
- **The Clurichen.** Reginald the frog warned you to avoid this trickster, which roams the town each night. What it's doing here remains unknown.
- **The black dragon.** It took George the woodsman's arm. Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold for its body, and you're considering the hunt. Sir Phidipaldi insulted Eric and slapped him with a glove.
- **Lady Hilda.** She offered to join your dragon hunt for pay but hasn't named a fee. She has chain mail and a shield, though she says she can't kill the dragon herself.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** This thief offered himself and his killer wasp for hire at a hundred gold a week.
- **The missing shipments.** Bentley and Farmer Gill told you no goods have reached Brandonsford in months. The reason is still unknown.
- **Shamus, Farmer Gill's prize mule.** Gill will sell him to Dunk for a hundred gold, saying he'd let Dunk start a farm. Helping kill the dragon might also win him, though Gill never agreed.
- **Quinn's deal.** Quinn the tavern owner blames Bentley for his ale vanishing nightly, though what empties it is unknown. For a hundred gold, Sir Phidipaldi must help stage a fake delivery of water-filled bottles.

## Where you are

Sir Phidipaldi is in the empty Golden Egg Tavern with Quinn. Across the street, Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is out at Farmer Gill's farm, drinking with Gill on the porch, with plans to sleep in the barn.

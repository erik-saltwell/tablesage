# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: "Ow, why did you do that?" - Squints**

## Last session...
- Father William marked the witch's home and the Barrow Mound on Dr. Uriah's treasure map.
- Trout recruited the halfling Squints into his Halfling Corps. Squints rescued Brother Dirk and gained him as a follower.
- The party caught Naggeneen, an invisible Clurichen stealing Quinn's alcohol, and Quinn hung him behind his bar.
- Dunk spotted a glowing, magical mithril chain shirt in Warwick the smith's shop at night.
- Dunk struck a deal with Ingrid to make a sleeping potion, then gave her heavily watered-down faun wine.
- Woodsman George told how the dragon killed his friends and doubted that Eric the Reeve has the bounty.
- Father William told the party that Brother Dirk went for Brandon's sword, and the party set out for the Barrow.
- Dr. Uriah struck a bargain with Father William to get the Barrow Mound and the witch's home marked on his map.
- The party snatched Brother Dirk from a sleeping giant's cottage and outran the giant across the field.

## Your goals
- Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Find the treasure shown on the party's treasure map.
- Dunk is considering acquiring Farmer Gill's prize mule Shamus, which Gill says would let him start his own farm.

## Standing in your way
- George says nothing pierces the dragon's black scales. Its poisonous bite kills, and it spits sulfur.

## What could help
- Father William says the magic sword buried with Brandon lies in the Barrow Mound, and the party is walking there to get it.
- George says the dragon's lair is up north at the foot of the mountains, with no climbing needed.
- Father William says the dragon can only be killed if the party gets Brandon's sword.
- If Dunk gets the magical mithril chain shirt in Warwick's shop, Gill says it would fit a normal-sized person but not Dunk. Gill says Warwick always refuses to sell it.
- If the party stops on the way north to the Barrow, they can check one location marked on the treasure map.
- Dunk says that if the party gets Warwick's shimmering armor, it could help against the dragon.
- If the party pays Lady Hilda an unnamed fee, she will join the dragon hunt. She says she cannot kill the dragon herself.
- If Dunk pays Farmer Gill 100 gold, Gill will sell him Shamus. Gill says he will lower the price once his boys come back.
- When Dunk offered work for Shamus, Farmer Gill said the big work needed was killing the dragon. No deal was agreed.

## Still unanswered
- Is Brandon's fairy-given sword real, and is it in the Barrow Mound?
- Where did this black dragon come from?
- What happened to the shipments meant for Brandonsford?

## Promises and debts
- Dunk must meet Ingrid tonight and help her get into Warwick's house and dose him, in return for the sleeping potion.
- Dr. Uriah owes Father William penance at the Barrow Mound for marking his map.
- The party owes Squints second pick of its first treasure, after the elite members. Until then, Squints funds himself and takes Trout's orders.
- Sir Phidipaldi told Lady Hilda the party would talk with Eric the Reeve and then come back to take up her services.

## Meanwhile
- George says the black dragon cannot fly yet but soon will, and then the town is doomed. Gill's three sons are out hunting it.
- A man-eating giant lives in a stone cottage on the party's route north. He is unharmed, and the party just robbed him and broke his window.

## Where you are
- The party is in the woods north of town, just past the field around the giant's stone cottage. Squints and the freed Brother Dirk are with them.

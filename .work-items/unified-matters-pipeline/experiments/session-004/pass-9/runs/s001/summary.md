# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map to places they didn't recognize.
- Reginald the frog warned the party to avoid a trickster Clurichen that comes from the riverbanks nightly.
- Eric the Reeve offered 1000 gold for the dragon. Sir Phidipaldi then challenged him to a duel, which Eric didn't accept.
- Lady Hilda offered to join the dragon hunt for pay, and Sir Phidipaldi said they would return.
- Dr. Uriah met Drop Dead Ned, a thief with a killer wasp, who offered himself for hire.
- Bentley and Farmer Gill told the party that supplies have stopped reaching Brandonsford.
- Dunk heard Farmer Gill's legend of Sir Brandon and kept him drinking on his porch all evening.
- Quinn offered Sir Phidipaldi 100 gold to help catch whoever drains the Golden Egg's ale each night.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure marked on the party's map.
- Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Find out who empties Quinn's bar at the Golden Egg each night and collect Quinn's 100 gold.

## What could help
- If Sir Phidipaldi makes a show of delivering Quinn's water-filled bottles as ale, they may see who comes for the stock.
- If the party goes back to the Clumsy Fox and pays Lady Hilda, she will join their dragon hunt.
- If the party pays Drop Dead Ned 100 gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do anything that lets him kill. He refunds 5 gold per kill if he can do what he wants with the bodies.

## Still unanswered
- Who or what empties Quinn's bar each night?
- Why does Warwick the smith keep adding lucky charms and growing more paranoid?
- What is stopping shipments from reaching Brandonsford?
- What does the Clurichen do in town each night?

## Meanwhile
- Farmer Gill's three sons are already hunting the dragon, and Gill expects them to bring it back. They could claim Eric's bounty first.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is in the empty Golden Egg Tavern with its owner Quinn. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm on the edge of town, where Gill has said he can sleep in the barn.
- Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn are about to stage a fake ale delivery for Quinn's promised 100 gold. Quinn will bring bottles to fill with water, and Sir Phidipaldi will make a big show of delivering them to see what happens.

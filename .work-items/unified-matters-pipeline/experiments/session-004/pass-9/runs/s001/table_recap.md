# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

- **The treasure map.** It led you to Brandonsford at sunset, but the places it marks are ones you don't recognize. The treasure itself is still unfound.
- **The Clurichen.** Reginald the frog warned you to avoid this trickster, which comes up from the riverbanks every night. What it does in town is still unknown.
- **Eric the Reeve's bounty.** He offers a thousand gold for the black dragon near Brandonsford, brought into town dead. He didn't accept Sir Phidipaldi's duel challenge. Farmer Gill's three sons are already hunting it.
- **Lady Hilda.** She offered to join your dragon hunt for pay, and Sir Phidipaldi said you'd be back. If you return to the Clumsy Fox and pay her, she'll join.
- **Brandonsford's missing supplies.** Bentley and Farmer Gill say shipments have stopped reaching town. What's stopping them is still unknown.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** Dr. Uriah met this thief and his killer wasp. For a hundred gold a week, he'll do anything that lets him kill, refunding five gold per kill if the bodies are his.
- **Warwick the smith.** He keeps adding lucky charms and growing more paranoid, and nobody knows why yet.
- **Quinn's vanishing ale.** Quinn, owner of the Golden Egg, offered Sir Phidipaldi a hundred gold to catch whoever drains the bar each night. Who or what does it is still unknown.
- **Dunk and Farmer Gill.** Dunk heard Gill's legend of Sir Brandon and kept him drinking on his porch all evening.

## Where you are

Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn, in the empty Golden Egg, are about to stage a fake delivery of water-filled bottles as ale. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street at the Clumsy Fox. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm, where he can sleep in the barn.

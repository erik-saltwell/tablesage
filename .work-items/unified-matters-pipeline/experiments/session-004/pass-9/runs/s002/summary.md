# Brandonsford

- The party caught Naggeneen, the Clurichen stealing Quinn's alcohol, and handed him over to Quinn.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure on the party's map.
- Kill the black dragon and drag its body into Brandonsford to collect Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Acquire Warwick the smith's mithril chain shirt.

## Standing in your way
- George says nothing pierces the dragon's black scales, so ordinary arrows and swords cannot kill it.
- Farmer Gill says Warwick always refuses to sell his mithril chain shirt. It fits a normal-sized person but not Dunk.

## What could help
- If Dunk helps Ingrid give Warwick her sleeping potion, Warwick should stay asleep while they are in his house. It may not work, since Dunk diluted the faun wine and Father William doubts Ingrid's skill.
- Father William says that if the party retrieves the sword buried in Brandon's Barrow Mound, they can kill the dragon. George says no one knows the truth or where the sword is.
- If the party hires Lady Hilda at the Clumsy Fox, the seasoned fighter will join their dragon hunt for pay.
- If the party pays Drop Dead Ned 100 gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do anything that lets him kill. He refunds 5 gold per kill if he can keep the bodies.
- If the party climbs through Warwick's cracked-open second-story bedroom window, they can reach the magical mithril chain shirt inside.
- If Ingrid's sleeping potion keeps Warwick asleep, Dunk could take the mithril chain shirt.

## Still unanswered

- Why are once-hidden fairies now coming out more often?

## Promises and debts

- Dr. Uriah must do penance at Brandon's Barrow Mound and donate 10 gold for its upkeep.

## Meanwhile

- George says the dragon cannot fly yet but soon will, and then the town is doomed.
- Farmer Gill's three sons are already hunting the dragon and could claim Eric's bounty first.
- A slow, man-eating giant lives in a stone cottage on the party's route to the Barrow. He is awake and saw the party steal his captive.

## Where you are

- The party is heading to the Barrow and trying to get Brother Dirk to come along, though Dirk says he should just head home. Trout has told Dirk he will report to Squints.

# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

- **The treasure map.** You followed it to Brandonsford, arriving at sunset, though you don't recognize the places it shows. The treasure it marks is still yours to find.
- **The black dragon.** It's not big. Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold for its body, though he declined Sir Phidipaldi's duel challenge. Lady Hilda at the Clumsy Fox will join you for pay.
- **The missing shipments.** Bentley, Farmer Gill and Quinn of the Golden Egg say supplies have stopped reaching town, leaving the taverns and the smith short. No one knows what became of them.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** For a hundred gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do any job. He insists on killing people, and gives back five gold for each one he may kill.
- **Quinn's vanishing ale.** Someone or something empties his bar every night, and he blames Bentley. He's hired Sir Phidipaldi for a hundred gold to get back at Bentley.

## Where you are

Sir Phidipaldi is in the empty Golden Egg with Quinn. He's agreed to make a big show of delivering water-filled bottles to see who's stealing. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street at the Clumsy Fox. Dunk is drinking with Farmer Gill on his porch at the edge of town.

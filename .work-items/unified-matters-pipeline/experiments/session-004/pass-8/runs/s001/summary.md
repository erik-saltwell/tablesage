# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map of places they did not recognize.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty for the black dragon. Sir Phidipaldi then insulted Eric and challenged him to a duel, which Eric declined.
- Bentley, Farmer Gill and Quinn said supplies have stopped reaching Brandonsford, leaving the taverns and smith short.
- Quinn hired Sir Phidipaldi to get back at Bentley, whom Quinn blames for stealing his ale.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure marked on the party's treasure map.
- Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect Quinn's 100 gold.

## What could help
- If Sir Phidipaldi stages a showy fake delivery of Quinn's water-filled bottles, they may see who comes for the stock at night.
- If the party returns to the Clumsy Fox and pays Lady Hilda, the seasoned warrior will join them against the dragon.
- If the party hires Drop Dead Ned for 100 gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do any job. Ned insists on killing people and gives back 5 gold for each one he may kill.

## Still unanswered

## Meanwhile

## Where you are

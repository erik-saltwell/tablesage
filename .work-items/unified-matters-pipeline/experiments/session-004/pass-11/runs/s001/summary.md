# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map that marks places they did not recognize.
- Reginald the frog warned the party that a trickster Clurichen comes from the riverbanks every night and to stay away from it.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty to whoever kills the black dragon troubling Brandonsford. Farmer Gill says his three sons are already hunting it.
- Lady Hilda offered to join a dragon hunt for pay, and Sir Phidipaldi said they would return after seeing the Reeve.
- Drop Dead Ned offered himself and his killer wasp for hire, and Dr. Uriah said he would think about it.
- Bentley said his tavern's restock shipment never arrived, and Farmer Gill said nothing has reached town in months.
- Sir Phidipaldi slapped Eric the Reeve with his glove and challenged him to a duel at dawn. Bentley banished him to the Golden Egg for the night.
- Dunk split from the party, and Farmer Gill told him the legend of Sir Brandon over drinks from his still.
- Quinn hired Sir Phidipaldi to help get back at Bentley, whom Quinn blames for his vanishing ale.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure.
- Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect Quinn's 100 gold.
- Duel Eric the Reeve at dawn, if Sir Phidipaldi still means to press his challenge.

## Standing in your way
- Eric the Reeve, offended by Sir Phidipaldi's false claims and glove slap, never accepted the dawn duel.

## What could help
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join their dragon hunt.
- If the party hires Drop Dead Ned for 100 gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do any job that lets him kill. He will refund 5 gold per kill if he can do what he wants with the bodies.
- If Sir Phidipaldi makes a big show of delivering Quinn's water-filled bottles, the fake ale shipment can bait Bentley.

## Still unanswered
- Why has nothing come into Brandonsford for months?
- Who or what empties Quinn's good stock every night?

## Meanwhile
- The black dragon that tore off the woodsman George's arm may maim others who meet it.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is at the Golden Egg Tavern with its owner Quinn. Wayman and Dr. Uriah are across the street at the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm on the edge of town, where Gill has welcomed him to sleep in the barn.
- Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn are setting up a fake ale shipment to catch Bentley. Quinn is to bring bottles to fill with water, and Sir Phidipaldi will make a big show of delivering them.

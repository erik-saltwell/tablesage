- **Dr. Uriah's promise to Father William.** He agreed to give penance at the Barrow Mound and to donate ten gold for its upkeep.
- **The giant's stone cottage.** Squints pulled Brother Dirk, a captive, out of it, and you outran the waking giant. The man-eater is unharmed and may catch and eat anyone passing near.
- **The fauns' wine.** Dunk drank it without effect, then traded a rabbit's foot for a faun's flask of it.
- **Dr. Uriah and Ingrid.** They agreed that Father William is the worst. Ingrid denied his claim about her potions.

## Where you are

You are in the woods north of town, across the field from the giant's stone cottage, with Squints and the rescued Brother Dirk. You are heading for the Barrow and pressing Dirk to come along rather than go home. Trout has told him he will report to Squints.

# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: Father William measuring Uriah's hands and feet with a ruler**

- **The treasure map.** Father William the priest marked the witch's home and Brandon's Barrow Mound on it. You plan to check one spot on the way to the Barrow. Which holds the treasure is unknown.
- **The Brandonsford dragon.** Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold for its body. George says its lair lies north by the mountains, arrows and swords can't pierce its scales, and it will soon fly.
- **The Clurichen.** Trout and Dr. Uriah caught the invisible Naggeneen with ink, and Quinn hung him behind the bar.
- **Lady Hilda.** She's a seasoned fighter staying at the Clumsy Fox, and she'll join the dragon hunt if you hire her.
- **Brandonsford's missing shipments.** Nobody yet knows why they've stopped reaching town.
- **Drop Dead Ned's offer.** He's a murderous thief who'll join you for a hundred gold a week if he gets to kill people. Dr. Uriah is thinking it over.
- **Squints and the Halfling Corps.** Trout recruited Squints as his junior assistant. Squints gets second pick of your first treasure, after the Corps' elite members.
- **Warwick's mithril chain shirt.** Dunk found it, glowing with magic, in the shop of Warwick the smith, and says he has a plan to get it. Warwick sleeps upstairs with his bedroom window cracked open.
- **Ingrid's sleeping potion.** Dunk agreed to help Ingrid dose Warwick with it so he can search for the shirt unnoticed. It isn't finished, and Dunk secretly diluted the faun wine he gave her, which may weaken it.
- **Brandon's sword at the Barrow.** Father William says the dragon can't be killed without it, though George says no one knows if the legend is true. You told Dunk to meet you there.
- **Uriah's penance.** Father William asked Dr. Uriah to give penance at Brandon's Barrow Mound, and he still owes it.
- **The giant in the woods.** Squints pulled Brother Dirk, bound, from its sack, and you outran it as it woke. It still lives on your route north, though it's slow.
- **Father William's measurements.** He measured Dr. Uriah's hands and feet for webbing, and found none.
- **The fauns.** Dunk astonished them by drinking their wine unaffected, then traded a child's rabbit's foot for a flask.
- **Dr. Uriah and Ingrid.** They bonded over disliking Father William. She said hidden fairies are appearing more often.

## Where you are

You stand in the forest north of town, across the field from the giant's stone cottage, with Squints and the freed Brother Dirk. You're bound for the Barrow and pressing Dirk to come along rather than go home. Trout has told him he'll report to Squints. Dirk hasn't answered.

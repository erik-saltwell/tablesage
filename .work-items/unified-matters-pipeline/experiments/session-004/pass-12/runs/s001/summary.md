# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

## Last session...
- The party arrived at Brandonsford following a treasure map whose marked locations they don't recognize.
- Reginald the Frog warned that a trickster Clurichen comes into town from the riverbanks every night.
- Lady Hilda offered to join the dragon hunt for pay, and Sir Phidipaldi said they would return.
- Bentley told the party his tavern's shipment never arrived, a loss hurting all of Brandonsford.
- Dunk drank with Farmer Gill, who offered him a mule and told how Sir Brandon once slew a dragon.
- Eric the Reeve offered a reward for killing the black dragon and dragging its body into town.
- Drop Dead Ned offered to hire on with his killer wasp, and Dr. Uriah said he'd think about it.
- Sir Phidipaldi agreed to help Quinn stage a fake ale delivery to catch whoever empties his bar.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure marked on the party's treasure map.
- Kill the black dragon and drag its body into Brandonsford to collect Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Catch whoever empties the Golden Egg's bar at night and collect Quinn's 100 gold.

## What could help
- If Sir Phidipaldi makes a big show of delivering water-filled bottles to the Golden Egg, it may lure whoever empties the bar.
- Eric the Reeve says woodsman George may come to the Clumsy Fox tonight. If the party questions him, they can learn where he met the dragon.
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join their dragon hunt.
- If the party pays Drop Dead Ned 100 gold a week, he and his killer wasp will join them. He insists on getting to kill people.

## Still unanswered
- Who or what empties the Golden Egg's stock every night?
- Where are the treasure map's marked locations, and which holds the treasure?
- Where can the black dragon be found?
- Why have shipments stopped reaching Brandonsford?

## Meanwhile
- The black dragon that took woodsman George's arm remains loose around Brandonsford and may maim those who confront it.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is at the Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Wayman and Dr. Uriah are at the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm on the edge of town, drinking with Gill on the porch.
- Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn are about to stage a fake ale shipment for Quinn's offer of 100 gold. Quinn will bring bottles, they will fill them with water, and Sir Phidipaldi will make a big show of delivering them to see what happens.

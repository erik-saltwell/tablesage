# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

- **The treasure map.** You followed it to Brandonsford, arriving at sunset, though the places it shows are unfamiliar to you. The treasure is still to be found.
- **The Clurichen of the riverbanks.** Reginald warned you that this trickster comes into town from the riverbanks every night. What it does here is still unknown.
- **The black dragon.** Eric the Reeve offers one thousand gold for its body, dragged into town. It tore off George the woodsman's arm, and Farmer Gill's three sons are hunting it now.
- **Lady Hilda's offer.** She will join your fight against the dragon if you pay her.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** He offered to work for Dr. Uriah, along with his killer wasp, for one hundred gold a week.
- **The missing shipments.** Bentley and Farmer Gill told you the town's tavern and smithy are running short. Why nothing has reached Brandonsford in months remains unknown.
- **Sir Phidipaldi's duel.** He challenged Eric the Reeve to fight at dawn, but Eric has not accepted.
- **Quinn's vanishing ale.** Quinn, owner of the Golden Egg, blames Bentley for stealing it and offers one hundred gold for revenge. Who or what empties his stock each night is still unknown.
- **Farmer Gill's tale.** Over drinks, he told Dunk how Sir Brandon slew a dragon long ago.

## Where you are

Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn are at the Golden Egg, about to fill bottles with water and stage a showy fake ale delivery. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street at the Clumsy Fox. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm, planning to sleep in the barn.

# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map to places they did not recognize.
- Reginald warned the party that a trickster called a Clurichen comes from the riverbanks into town every night.
- Eric the Reeve, who runs Brandonsford for the common people, offered a bounty to whoever kills the rumored black dragon.
- Lady Hilda offered to join the party's fight against the dragon for pay.
- Drop Dead Ned offered to work for Dr. Uriah with his killer wasp for 100 gold a week.
- Bentley and Farmer Gill told the party that missing shipments have left Brandonsford's tavern and smithy short.
- Sir Phidipaldi challenged Eric the Reeve to a duel, and Bentley sent him to the Golden Egg for the night.
- Dunk drank with Farmer Gill, who told him how Sir Brandon slew a dragon long ago.
- Quinn, who owns the Golden Egg, blamed Bentley for stealing his ale and offered a reward for revenge.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure.
- Kill the black dragon near Brandonsford and drag its body into town for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect Quinn's 100 gold.
- Have Sir Phidipaldi fight Eric the Reeve in a duel at dawn.

## Standing in your way
- Eric the Reeve has not accepted Sir Phidipaldi's duel challenge.

## What could help
- If the party stages a fake ale delivery with water-filled bottles, they can see who comes to take it.
- Eric says the woodsman George may come to the Clumsy Fox tonight. If the party asks him, they can learn what he saw and where.
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join them against the dragon.

## Still unanswered
- Who or what empties Quinn's stock each night?
- What is the Clurichen doing in Brandonsford every night?
- Why has nothing reached Brandonsford in months?

## Meanwhile
- The black dragon tore off the woodsman George's arm, and Farmer Gill's three sons are hunting it now.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is at the Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street at the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm on the edge of town, where he plans to sleep in the barn.
- Sir Phidipaldi and Quinn are about to stage a fake ale delivery. Quinn will bring bottles, they will fill them with water, and Sir Phidipaldi will make a big show of delivering them.

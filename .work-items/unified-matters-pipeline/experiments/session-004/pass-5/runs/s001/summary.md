# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map to places they did not recognize.
- Reginald warned of a Clurichen trickster that comes into town from the riverbanks each night.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty for the dragon. Sir Phidipaldi then slapped him and challenged him to a duel, which Eric did not accept.
- Lady Hilda offered to join the party for pay, and Sir Phidipaldi said they would come back for her.
- Drop Dead Ned offered himself and his killer wasp for hire, and Dr. Uriah said he would think about it.
- Bentley and Gill told the party that supplies have stopped reaching Brandonsford.
- Quinn promised Sir Phidipaldi 100 gold to help him get back at Bentley, whom he blames for stolen ale.

## Your goals
- Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect his promised 100 gold.
- Find the treasure the party's map leads to.
- Kill the black dragon and drag its body into Brandonsford for Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold bounty.
- Get Farmer Gill's prize mule Shamus so Dunk can start his own farm.
- Acquire Warwick the smith's mithril chain shirt, which would fit a normal-sized person but not Dunk.

## Standing in your way
- Gill says Warwick always refuses to sell the mithril chain shirt he displays in his shop.

## What could help
- If Sir Phidipaldi makes a show of delivering Quinn's water-filled bottles to the Golden Egg, they can see what happens.
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join them to hunt the dragon.
- If the party agrees on a fee, Lady Hilda will join the hunt, though she says she cannot kill the dragon herself.
- If Dunk helps kill the dragon, Gill might trade Shamus for it, though Gill never agreed to a deal.

## Still unanswered
- What empties Quinn's stock every night?
- What happened to the shipments that stopped reaching Brandonsford?
- Where did the black dragon come from?

## Meanwhile
- A small black dragon roams outside Brandonsford and took the woodsman George's arm. Gill's three sons are out trying to kill it.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is at the bar of the empty Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm, drinking with Gill on the porch before bedding down in the barn.

# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: Sir Phidipaldi slapping the Reeve with his glove**

- **The treasure map.** It led you to Brandonsford at sunset, toward places you didn't recognize. The treasure it points to is still waiting to be found.
- **The Clurichen trickster.** Reginald warned that it comes into town from the riverbanks each night.
- **The dragon bounty.** Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold if you kill the small black dragon and drag its body into town. Sir Phidipaldi slapped him and challenged him to a duel, which Eric declined.
- **Lady Hilda.** She will join your hunt for an agreed fee, though she says she cannot kill the dragon herself. Sir Phidipaldi told her you would come back for her.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** He offered himself and his killer wasp for hire, and Dr. Uriah said he would think about it.
- **The missing shipments.** Bentley and Gill told you that supplies have stopped reaching Brandonsford. What happened to them is still unknown.
- **Shamus the mule.** Dunk wants Farmer Gill's prize mule to start a farm. Gill might trade him for Dunk's help killing the dragon, but never agreed. Gill's three sons are out hunting it.
- **Warwick's mithril shirt.** Warwick the smith displays it in his shop, but Gill says he always refuses to sell it. It would fit a normal-sized person, though not Dunk.
- **Quinn's stolen ale.** Quinn blames Bentley and promised Sir Phidipaldi a hundred gold for revenge. Something empties his stock nightly. Showily delivering his water-filled bottles to the Golden Egg would show what happens.

## Where you are

Sir Phidipaldi stands at the bar of the empty Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Across the street, Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is drinking with Farmer Gill on his porch before bedding down in the barn.

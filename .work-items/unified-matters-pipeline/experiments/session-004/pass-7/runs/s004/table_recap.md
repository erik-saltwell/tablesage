# Brandonsford
**2026-09-22: Drop Dead Ned's blood-red wasp spinning inside Sir Alfred's ribs**

- **The treasure map.** It came from the dwarf Bral and also marks the Dwarven mine. The ghost of Bral's brother Grimny says Bral probably hid the dwarves' hoard in the Dragon's Lair.
- **The black dragon.** Eric the Reeve pays a thousand gold for its body. The witch Vivian says enough faun wine would knock it out, so you could drive Sir Brandon's sword through its mouth.
- **Your new companions.** Lady Hilda and the wizard Malzazarik joined you. You owe them pay, pledged against Cedric's promissory note.
- **Drop Dead Ned.** He travels with you as a hired scout for a hundred gold. He carries tools such as a crowbar.
- **The missing shipments.** Cedric and Warwick the smith described their lost supplies, and Warwick pointed you to a mine in the north. It's still unknown what is stopping shipments into Brandonsford, including Bentley's overdue restock.
- **Sir Phidipaldi's quarrel with Eric the Reeve.** He challenged the Reeve to a duel at dawn, and it is still to be fought.
- **Shamus the mule.** Farmer Gill asked a hundred gold for him and says he may go lower. He won't lower it until his boys return, since he can't work without them or a mule.
- **Warwick's mithril shirt.** Wayman wears it on loan. It halves damage from magic weapons and spells. Warwick holds Sir Phidipaldi's armor and lyre as collateral, and you owe him metal from the Dwarven mine.
- **Squints the halfling.** He joined you as Trout's junior assistant. Trout owes him a pick from the Halfling Corps' first treasure after the elite members, and until then he pays his own way.
- **Dr. Uriah's penance.** He owes Father William penance at the Barrow Mound and a ten gold donation for its upkeep.
- **The hidden fairies.** They were once hidden, and no one yet knows why they now come out more often.
- **The giant in the cyclopean cottage.** This slow, man-eating giant north of Brandonsford is now awake. He chased you until he tired.
- **The undead swordsman of the Barrow Mound.** Drop Dead Ned's wasp broke apart Sir Alfred, the undead knight. You left him incapacitated but not destroyed.
- **The Free Goblin Society.** Wayman persuaded the goblins to wall off the mound's upper rooms and destroy its church. You later trailed drunk goblins to King Hogboon's temple castle.
- **The barrow robes.** Dr. Uriah kept them through a deal with Brother Dirk, but they are not magical. He promised to return them to the church once the dragon is defeated.
- **The voice in the tomb.** No one knows whose female voice asked, "What makes a true knight?"
- **Cedric's promissory note.** At Sir Phidipaldi's insistence, Cedric signed it for your barrow treasure. Dr. Uriah tallies it at nine hundred seventy-five gold, payable once the caravans flow again.
- **Dunk's dwarven ring.** He took this magic ring from a dead dwarf in the cave-in, and wearing it, he met Grimny's ghost.
- **Grimny's murder.** His ghost believes greedy Bral murdered him, but how he died is still unknown. Dunk suspects there is no dragon at all, only Bral pretending.

## Where you are

You stand at the caved-in entrance of the Dwarven mine, beside the burnt cabin at the base of the hill, with Malzazarik the Magnificent and Lady Hilda. The collapse looks natural, and it can be dug through.

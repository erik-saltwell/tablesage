# Brandonsford
**2026-08-18: "I challenge thee to a duel at dawn" - Sir Phidipaldi**

## Last session...
- The party reached Brandonsford at sunset, following a treasure map to places they did not recognize.
- Eric the Reeve offered a bounty for the black dragon rumored around Brandonsford. Farmer Gill's three sons are already hunting it.
- Sir Phidipaldi told Lady Hilda the party would hire her after speaking with the Reeve.
- Dr. Uriah met Drop Dead Ned, a murderous thief who keeps a killer wasp in a jar.
- Bentley and Farmer Gill said no supplies have reached Brandonsford in months.
- Sir Phidipaldi slapped Eric the Reeve and challenged him to a duel at dawn. Bentley banned him from the Clumsy Fox for the night.
- Sir Phidipaldi agreed to help Golden Egg owner Quinn get back at rival tavern keeper Bentley.

## Your goals
- Find the treasure marked on the party's map.
- Kill the black dragon and drag its body into Brandonsford to collect Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Help Quinn get back at Bentley and collect Quinn's 100 gold.
- Fight Eric the Reeve in Sir Phidipaldi's duel at dawn.
- Acquire the mithril chain shirt Warwick the smith displays, if anyone in the party wants it.

## Standing in your way
- Farmer Gill says Warwick always refuses to sell his mithril chain shirt.

## What could help
- If the party finds George the woodsman, who may come to the Clumsy Fox tonight, he can describe the dragon that took his arm.
- If the party hires Lady Hilda, she will help them fight the dragon.
- If the party pays Lady Hilda, she will join them.
- If the party pays Drop Dead Ned 100 gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do anything that lets him kill people.
- If the party fills Quinn's bottles with water and Sir Phidipaldi makes a show of delivering them, Quinn gets his fake ale shipment.
- The party has a treasure map marking several unfamiliar locations near Brandonsford.

## Still unanswered

- What empties or knocks over Quinn's stock every night?

## Meanwhile
- The black dragon that tore off George's arm is still at large around Brandonsford and may maim others.

## Where you are
- Sir Phidipaldi is at the empty Golden Egg Tavern with its owner, Quinn. Wayman Tisdale and Dr. Uriah are across the street in the Clumsy Fox Tavern. Dunk is at Farmer Gill's farm on the edge of town, staying the night in the barn.

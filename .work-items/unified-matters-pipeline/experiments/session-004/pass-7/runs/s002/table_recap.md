# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: The invisible Clurichen slipping on Phidipaldi's marbles**

- **The treasure map.** It marks several unrecognized places near Brandonsford, and you still seek its treasure. Checking a marked spot on your way north through the forest could turn it up.
- **Naggeneen the Clurichen.** You caught the invisible thief who was stealing Quinn's booze and handed him over to Quinn.
- **The black dragon.** Eric the Reeve offers a thousand gold for its body, though George the woodsman doubts he can pay. George says nothing pierces its scales, and it can't fly yet.
- **Lady Hilda.** She's a seasoned fighter in chain mail with a shield, and if paid she'll fight the dragon beside you. Sir Phidipaldi must return for her after you talk to the Reeve.
- **Drop Dead Ned's offer.** For a hundred gold a week, he and his killer wasp will do anything that lets him kill people.
- **Brandonsford's missing shipments.** Nothing has reached the town in months, and why is still a mystery.
- **Sir Phidipaldi's duel.** He has challenged Eric the Reeve to a duel at dawn, and that fight is still ahead of him.
- **Shamus the mule.** You want him from Farmer Gill, who won't lower his price until his boys return. Without them or a mule, Gill can't work.
- **Warwick's mithril chain shirt.** Dunk spotted the glowing magical armor at Warwick the smith's shop and says he has a plan to get it. Farmer Gill says Warwick always refuses to sell it.
- **Trout's Halfling Corps.** Trout recruited Squints, a halfling, as junior assistant, and put Brother Dirk under him. Squints pays his own way until he picks from the Corps' first treasure, after the elite members.
- **Ingrid's sleeping potion.** Dunk gave Ingrid the Alchemist a watered-down batch of faun wine. He can collect the potion tonight. In return, he must help her into Warwick's house to dose him.
- **Sir Brandon's sword.** Father William says it lies buried with Sir Brandon in his Barrow Mound, and you've set out for it. It is said to be the only way to kill the dragon.
- **Dr. Uriah's penance.** He must do penance at the Barrow Mound and give Father William ten gold for its upkeep.
- **The hidden fairies.** They once stayed hidden but now come out more often, and why is still a mystery.
- **The giant in the cyclopean cottage.** Squints pulled Brother Dirk from the sleeping giant's sack, and you outran it across a field when it woke. Dirk says it meant to stew him.

## Where you are

You stand in the field near the giant's cyclopean cottage, in the woods north of Brandonsford. Squints and the freed Brother Dirk are with you.

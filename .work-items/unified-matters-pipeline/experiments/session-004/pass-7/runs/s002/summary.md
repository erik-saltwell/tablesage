# Brandonsford
**2026-08-25: The invisible Clurichen slipping on Phidipaldi's marbles**

## Last session...
- Trout recruited Squints, a newly arrived halfling, as junior assistant in his Halfling Corps. Trout later put the rescued Brother Dirk under Squints.
- The party caught Naggeneen, the invisible Clurichen stealing Quinn's booze, and handed him over to Quinn.
- Dunk spotted a glowing magical mithril chain shirt in Warwick the smith's shop and told the party he had a plan to get it.
- Dunk struck a deal with Ingrid the Alchemist for a sleeping potion. He got wine from the fauns and gave her a watered-down batch.
- George the woodsman described the dragon's deadly powers and lair, and doubted the Reeve can pay the bounty.
- The party set out for Sir Brandon's Barrow Mound after Father William pointed them to the sword buried there.
- Squints pulled Brother Dirk from a sleeping giant's sack, and the party outran the waking giant across a field.

## Your goals
- Acquire the mithril chain shirt from Warwick the smith's shop.
- Find the treasure marked on the party's treasure map.
- Kill the black dragon and drag its body into Brandonsford to collect Eric the Reeve's 1000 gold.
- Fight Eric the Reeve in the dawn duel Sir Phidipaldi challenged him to.
- Get Farmer Gill's mule Shamus.

## Standing in your way

- Farmer Gill won't lower his price for Shamus until his boys return, since he can't work without them or a mule.
- Farmer Gill says Warwick always refuses to sell his mithril chain shirt.

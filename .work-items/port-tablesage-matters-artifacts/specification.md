## Producers and Storage

Ledger retains `ledger.json`/`ledger.md` and its v5 application envelope (Session UUID/name, roster, optional bootstrap situation and utterances). It consumes current Role Transcript and Transcript Sections, attendees and Glossary. Only the first recorded Session uses the starting-context range. The packaged first-session schema requires `starting_situation`; continuation calls do not supply bootstrap context. Ledger never consumes prior Matters. Generation makes one validated call and records the source structural checks. Markdown uses the imported renderer; exporting Markdown does not rewrite the canonical pair.

Scene Log retains `scene_log.json`/`scene_log.md`, now using version 2 with application-owned Session UUID/name/predecessor UUID. Its generated content is exactly `scenes` and `session_cue`. Scenes contain location, participants (party members/NPCs with optional involvement notes), overview, scene cue, events, optional discoveries and outcome. Events/discoveries retain importance ratings. `session_cue` must exactly match a scene cue. Other imported structural checks are recorded as warnings. Context is the Ledger bootstrap situation or prior Present Matters rendered as a passage; prior open categories do not enter Scene Log generation. Attendees and Glossary are explicit inputs. Version 1 read types remain only for dormant preparation consumers.

Ten new hidden file artifacts are `present_matters`, `obligations`, `goals`, `brewing_troubles`, `goal_impediments`, `opportunities`, `mysteries`, `new_assets`, `revelations` and `developments`. Each `<category>.json` contains exactly its category key and stored result. Its required `<category>.evidence.json` companion retains raw and normalized unfiltered responses, model and effective options. Even rejected responses remain in `.generation-records/`, without becoming downstream state. Normal rollback-safe multi-file persistence and interruption markers apply. The subsequent [Post-Session Matters contract](../post-session-matters/specification.md) adds a visible deterministic combination of these publications.

Present Matters contains only `current_position` and `present_danger`. It does not generate immediate goals or next actions. Scene Log continuity uses only the prior position and danger. Existing publications containing `immediate_goals` require regeneration; no automatic migration is performed.

Developments titles and descriptions recount events in past tense, with the end of the Session as the reference point. Titles retain natural read-aloud wording, including articles and other small words. The deterministic Session Summary preserves the generated tense.

Opportunities require a nonempty `title` alongside `opening`, `action` and `advantage`. The title is one short, simple sentence combining those three details (or two short sentences when one would be crowded; amended 2026-10-28, see below), preserving essential conditions, attribution and uncertainty. Every generated entry, including carried and ended entries, receives a title. Existing entries without titles require regeneration; do not synthesize titles by concatenation or fall back to opening-only recap text. Filtering and ID normalization retain the title unchanged. Post-Session Matters copies it and Session Summary reads it as the Opportunity bullet.

Obligations require `commitment_type`: `general_agreement`, `personal_promise`, `debt`, `bargain`, `assigned_duty` or `oath`. General agreements include assent to an approach without an established duty owed to its proposer. Personal promises establish a specific undertaking on which the recipient can reasonably rely. Bargains cover commercial and noncommercial reciprocal exchanges, replacing the former `business_contract` value. Assigned duties are concrete, bounded responsibilities accepted under a role, office or authority. The LLM classifies all candidates, including general agreements, independently of burden, consequence, stake and lifecycle; unfiltered evidence retains those classifications. Where bases overlap, prefer an explicit oath, then repayment of an established debt, then a bargain, then an assigned duty, then a personal promise, otherwise general agreement. Ordinary promise/vow wording does not itself establish an oath. Existing entries missing the field or using `business_contract` require regeneration rather than an inferred default or automatic relabeling. The combined Post-Session Matters schema reuses this required field unchanged.

Obligations also require `stake`: `trivial`, `low`, `medium` or `high`, assessing the recipient's established interest in fulfillment of the specific agreed terms. The rubric is defined in the Obligations system prompt: trivial means no meaningful demonstrated interest or reliance; low means a modest preference or benefit; medium means a meaningful need or plan would suffer a material setback; high means a vital or deeply important interest depends on performance. Establish a recipient interest in or reliance on the specific performance before rating its magnitude. Speculative usefulness or a tactical observation alone does not establish stake. If the broader goal could be achieved without the promised performance, leaving no established recipient interest unmet and no reliance unserved, use `trivial`. Preserve actual reliance on agreed services such as deliveries and escorts despite theoretical alternatives. Assess the specific duty rather than importing the stakes of its broader goal or a suggested method. Keep stake independent of burden, consequence and commitment type. Unfiltered evidence and filtered publications retain the field unchanged; publication excludes `stake: trivial` and permits `low`, `medium` and `high` when the other filters also pass. Existing entries missing stake require regeneration, with no inferred default or backfill.

Revelations text preserves its concise headline structure and formatting, now in sentence case (amended 2026-10-28; see below), while retaining articles, pronouns, prepositions, auxiliary verbs and other words needed for natural reading. New Assets names and benefits retain their compact noun-phrase/sentence forms and existing formatting, with the same grammatical-word preservation. Compress by removing incidental content or tightening wording, never by dropping needed words; permit a few extra words without adding background or changing certainty.

## Dependencies and Filtering

`session_pipeline/bundles/campaign_matters.py` declares the six waves and exact current dependency sets. Every category consumes the full Scene Log. All except Developments consume party Roles. Prior inputs are exactly the corresponding stored category, except Revelations consumes prior Goals/Mysteries/Impediments/Obligations/Opportunities and no prior Revelations; New Assets consumes its own prior result plus prior Goals/Impediments/Opportunities; Developments has no prior input. Missing or stale required prior state blocks generation or is rebuilt through the graph. Only bootstrap uses explicit empty priors.

Obligations now runs in the fourth wave alongside Mysteries and New Assets, after current Goals, Goal Impediments and Opportunities have been published. It receives all three current filtered arrays as duplicate-avoidance context. The prompt excludes new obligations already represented in these inputs by meaning, while still reconciling every previously tracked obligation. Sharing an entity or broader objective alone is not duplication; a distinct, independently fulfillable duty remains eligible. These current dependencies participate in generation planning and freshness through the shared dependency declaration.

The complete raw response is schema-validated first. Goals/Impediments/Opportunities are normalized, then all applicable cross-category links are checked before filtering. Filtered state preserves entry order and every retained field, and is revalidated before publication. Both same-session and next-session consumers read the filtered publication:

| Category | Publication Policy |
|---|---|
| Present Matters | Keep everything |
| Obligations | Exclude `general_agreement` and trivial stake; outstanding; burden and consequence both nontrivial |
| Goals | Open, top-level adventure/side Goals |
| Brewing Troubles | Exclude party-origin harm and any Low evaluation; no lifecycle/development-kind exclusion |
| Goal Impediments | Active only |
| Opportunities | Available roots only |
| Mysteries | Open, open-ended; all four dimensions medium/high |
| New Assets | Exclude low/trivial support unless acquired from an Opportunity |
| Revelations | Reframing or substantial/resolved mystery progress; impact, strength and unexpectedness all medium/high |
| Developments | Keep everything |

The port removes the source's unused Mystery ID/calibration functions and assembled-document/historical-category adapters. Imported helper formatting/typing is adapted to repository conventions; normalizers additionally accept the Campaign high-water value. The standalone selection policies are otherwise preserved, except for the subsequent commitment-type and stake exclusions for Obligations described above.

## Validation Retry

All ten Matters producers allow one validation retry by default: invalid JSON, schema violations, and response normalization/ID/link errors trigger a fresh call with the same inputs, model, schema and options. A second invalid response raises the validation error. Each returned response is saved separately in `.generation-records/` before validation; published output and matching raw/normalized evidence are replaced only after success. Logs record attempt count and validation failures with evidence paths. Transport/authentication, cancellation and filesystem failures surface immediately. Permanent IDs reserved by rejected attempts remain consumed, as usual.

The deployed `generate_artifacts.matters_validation_retries` defaults to 1 and accepts only 0 (disabled) or 1. Application passes the plain value explicitly and fingerprints it for Matters generation; it is not a provider option. Existing settings use the default when the field is absent. Ledger, Scene Log and other producers retain their existing retry policies.

## Persistent IDs

The Campaign folder owns `.matter-ids.sqlite3`, a small allocation database keyed by category. Goal, Impediment and Opportunity namespaces remain `goal_<n>`, `impediment_<n>` and `opportunity_<n>`. Temporary model IDs are replaced in response order and intra-response links are rewritten. Existing supplied prior IDs must return unchanged. Unknown/duplicate IDs, omitted prior entries, invalid parents/cycles and invalid cross-category targets are rejected.

A `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction serializes allocation across processes. The allocation floor is the highest persisted counter, supplied prior ID or existing current/normalized evidence ID when bootstrapping the counter store. Reserve before publication so crashes, rejected links and discarded candidates can leave gaps but cannot reuse numbers. Session cleanup does not remove the Campaign counter. Campaign moves/exports retain the counter file with the rest of the Campaign folder. Allocation metadata is not a build dependency; a later allocation must not make prior artifacts stale.

## Generation Controls and Freshness

All Ledger/Scene Log/Matters calls honor `llm_model_high`. Effective options override `reasoning_effort` with deployed `generate_artifacts.narrative_effort: high` for Ledger/Scene Log and `generate_artifacts.matters_effort: medium` for Matters. The stage contract fixes those two values. Other configured options and the generation timeout remain in effect; parameter dropping is disabled for these calls so effort cannot be silently omitted. Provider rejection surfaces as a generation failure. Transcript Sections and optional Player Introductions keep their existing generation behavior.

Producer records capture inputs before generation, including prompt/template/schema files, effective controls and actual roster/Glossary/context values. Existing Transcript Sections and Player Introductions also declare their real post-role inputs. Pre-role processing is unchanged. Scene Log depends on previous Present Matters and predecessor identity. Each Matters node declares its exact current/prior artifact references. The generation planner computes downstream closure from these actual graph edges rather than maintaining a second static dependency map. Current-only regeneration cannot skip missing/stale prior prerequisites. Later Sessions become stale through those edges; requesting their generation rebuilds required earlier state. Independent categories can remain current. Python source files are not freshness dependencies for any producer. Code edits require explicit regeneration when their effects should be applied; obsolete Python fingerprints in existing records are ignored without migration.

**Read-aloud wording (amended 2026-10-28 by [Improve Phrasing](../summary-improve-phrasing/idea.md)).** Each Matters prompt now writes the primary field the Session Summary shows (Developments and Goals-family `title`, Revelations `text`, New Assets `name`, Present Matters `current_position` and `present_danger`) to be read aloud after its Summary heading: sentence case, and a standalone phrase that neither repeats nor continues the heading. Developments and Revelations headlines are therefore sentence case rather than title case. A briefly added rule addressing the party as "you" was reverted the same day at the user's direction; the party remains "the party".

**Plain wording (amended 2026-10-28 by [Clearer Matters Wording](../matters-plain-language/intent.md)).** The primary Summary fields listed above also follow four plain-wording rules: put the actor and action early; use familiar verbs and everyday words; split a crowded sentence into two short sentences within the same entry rather than stacking clauses; and keep attribution and uncertainty beside the claim, naming the source first. Simpler wording keeps every name, amount, condition, cause, source and uncertainty, and already clear entries are not rewritten. Consequently, the Opportunity title, the Brewing Trouble title, Present Matters entries, and Developments and Revelations headlines may use a second short sentence for an essential condition, consequence or attribution; explanatory follow-up sentences remain excluded. A Mystery title stays one question, Goals titles keep their verb-first form, and New Assets `name` may stay a noun phrase; non-Summary fields such as `benefit` are unchanged. When a Goal, Goal Impediment, Opportunity, Mystery, Obligation or Brewing Trouble carries forward with unchanged facts, terms and lifecycle, its title is copied word for word unless it breaks these rules. This is prompt guidance only; no code enforces it, and schemas, qualification, lifecycle and the Summary layout are unchanged.

# Session Summary: Reserve The Danger Right Now for Genuine Urgent Threats: Intent

When the Game Master opens the next Session, the Session Summary's **"The danger right now"** section (rendered from `present_matters.present_danger`) names a genuine threat active at the moment play resumes. The section is omitted when there is none.

## Quality rubric

Approved by the user on 2026-10-28. Assessments are qualitative judgments explained in words: no scores, scales, weights or thresholds.

**Subject:** the "The danger right now" content of a Session Summary, and the Present Matters `present_danger` entries it comes from, judged across Sessions.

**Intended effect:** the GM can resume play on the real immediate threat, or on none, without hooks or long-running troubles posing as danger.

### Dimensions

1. **Genuine urgency:** whatever is listed is a threat active at the moment play resumes, something the characters must face now. Hooks, mysteries, potential future risks and long-running troubles don't belong here.
2. **No missed threats:** when play stopped mid-crisis (a skeleton in the doorway, a room flooding), the danger is listed. This guards against the opposite failure to genuine urgency, since a stricter definition can drop real dangers.
3. **Faithful to what happened:** each danger states who is threatened and how close or severe it is, keeps established uncertainty, and doesn't raise the stakes, predict the outcome or suggest a way out.
4. **Fits with the other sections:** danger content doesn't repeat "As we return" or "Meanwhile". Anything excluded from this section still appears where it belongs: a hook like Dunk's possibly cursed ring shows up under "Still unanswered", for example, rather than vanishing from the Summary.

### Distinguishing examples

These clarify the dimensions; they are not assessments of the current output.

- An empty section after a mid-combat stop is strong on genuine urgency but weak on no missed threats.
- Listing the skeleton as "will kill you unless you flee" is strong on urgency and on no missed threats, but weak on faithfulness.
- Dropping the ring from the Summary entirely is strong on urgency but weak on fit with the other sections.

### Context

The user's original concern was a false positive: Session 004 listed Dunk's possibly cursed ring. "No missed threats" was kept as a separate dimension at the user's approval, because the obvious remedy, a stricter definition, risks the opposite failure.

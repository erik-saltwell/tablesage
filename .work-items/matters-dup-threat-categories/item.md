# Matters Duplication: One Threat Across Threat Categories

One threat from this Session is restated across Development, Present Danger, Brewing Trouble and Goal Impediment. Seen in two analysed Sessions, including the worst offender found (the Session 003 skeleton in six bullets). The goal is a Session Summary in which this repetition no longer occurs, while distinct information is preserved.

This is pattern P4 from the [duplication analysis](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/analysis.md) of the parent item [Reduce duplication across Matters within a Session](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/). See [Intent](intent.md) for the pattern shape, the Matters categories it shows up in, the observed occurrences, and the quality rubric.

Closed 2026-10-29 at the user's direction, without implementation.

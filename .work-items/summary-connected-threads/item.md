# Session Summary: connect related threads

Make related items reference each other so continuity becomes usable at the table (reincorporation and player agency):

- Pair an obstacle with any acquired resource or opening that answers it. In Sessions 003–004, "Ordinary weapons cannot pierce the Black Dragon's scales" stays unchanged even after the party acquired Sir Brandon's magic sword and Vivian proposed driving it through the dragon's mouth. Desired: "Ordinary weapons can't pierce its scales — but you have Sir Brandon's sword."

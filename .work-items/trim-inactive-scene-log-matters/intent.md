# Scene Log Endcap Cleanup

Use an LLM cleanup pass after Scene Log generation to remove Endcap matters that have become irrelevant as players drop them or move on. A matter being untouched during the current Session is not sufficient grounds for removal.

## Quality Rubric

Approved by the user. These dimensions judge whether cleanup reduces irrelevant Endcap content while preserving useful campaign continuity. They are qualitative dimensions, without numerical scores or anchors, as developed using the requested define-rubric skill.

- **Noise removal:** Removes matters whose relevance has faded as the campaign moved on.
- **Live-matter preservation:** Keeps matters that could still affect meaningful choices, including dormant goals, mysteries, obligations, threats, and opportunities.
- **Grounded judgment:** Bases removal decisions on established campaign context, preserving uncertainty and avoiding invented abandonment or resolution.

These dimensions are independently assessable: keeping everything can protect live matters while leaving noise; aggressive pruning can clear noise while losing useful continuity. A removal can happen to be appropriate while its supporting judgment is not grounded in campaign evidence.

# Session 2

## Opening Situation

Hero is alone at the mill doorway, investigating beside the empty iron hook where the brass key is missing.

## Scenes

### Brass key beneath a loose floorboard

- The brass key was missing from its iron hook by the mill doorway, where the bridge Guard had said it would be; Hero searched the mill and found it beneath a loose floorboard.

### Resting on the far bank at noon

- Hero unlocked the iron gate barring the old bridge with the brass key, crossed, and rested on the far bank at noon.

## Endcap

### End Situation

Hero is alone on the far bank of the old bridge, resting at noon.

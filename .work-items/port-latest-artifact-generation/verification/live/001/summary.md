# Session 1

## Opening Situation

At dawn, Hero stands at the old bridge, where a locked iron gate blocks the way; a guard says the brass key is in the mill.

## Scenes

### Locked iron gate at dawn

- At the old bridge, a locked iron gate stopped Hero from crossing.
- The Guard said the brass key is in the mill, and Hero resolved to find it and open the gate.

### Empty iron hook beside the doorway

- At the mill, Hero found an empty iron hook beside the doorway and no brass key, despite the Guard's claim.
- Hero stopped at the doorway and began investigating, with the key still not found.

## Endcap

### End Situation

Hero is alone at the mill doorway, investigating beside the empty iron hook where the brass key is missing.

### Active Goals

- **Cross the old bridge:** Hero wants to cross the old bridge, which is blocked by a locked iron gate that needs the brass key.

### Unresolved Mysteries

- **Whereabouts of the brass key:** Where is the brass key? The bridge Guard said it is in the mill, but the iron hook beside the mill doorway is empty and the key is missing.

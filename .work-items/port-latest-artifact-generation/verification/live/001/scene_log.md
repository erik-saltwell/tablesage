# Session 1: Scene Log

## Opening Situation

At dawn, Hero stands at the old bridge, where a locked iron gate blocks the way; a guard says the brass key is in the mill.

## Scenes

### Locked iron gate at dawn

**Location:** The old bridge

**Node:** An old bridge whose way is blocked by a locked iron gate, watched by a guard.

**Group:** Hero

**Goals:**
- Cross the bridge

**Obstacle:** A locked iron gate blocks the bridge.

**Approach:** Hero listens to the guard and resolves to find the brass key.

**Outcome:** Hero cannot pass and decides to seek the brass key to open the gate.

**Scene Moments:**
- **Cross the old bridge — opened:** The Guard said the brass key is in the mill, and Hero intends to find it to cross the bridge.

### Empty iron hook beside the doorway

**Location:** The mill

**Node:** The mill where the Guard said the brass key would be found.

**Group:** Hero

**Goals:**
- Find the brass key

**Obstacle:** The brass key is missing from the mill.

**Approach:** Hero stops at the mill doorway to investigate.

**Outcome:** Hero has not found the key and is beginning to investigate at the doorway.

**Scene Moments:**
- **Whereabouts of the brass key — opened:** An empty iron hook hangs beside the mill doorway, and the key is not there despite the Guard's claim.

## Endcap

### End Situation

Hero is alone at the mill doorway, investigating beside the empty iron hook where the brass key is missing.

### Active Goals

- **Cross the old bridge:** Hero wants to cross the old bridge, which is blocked by a locked iron gate that needs the brass key.

### Unresolved Mysteries

- **Whereabouts of the brass key:** Where is the brass key? The bridge Guard said it is in the mill, but the iron hook beside the mill doorway is empty and the key is missing.

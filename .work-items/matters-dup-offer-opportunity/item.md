# Matters Duplication: Offers and Plans Restated as Opportunities

A Development records an offer or plan, and an Opportunity restates it as something the party could do, sometimes after the party has already started. Seen in three analysed Sessions. The goal is a Session Summary in which this repetition no longer occurs, while distinct information is preserved.

This is pattern P2 from the [duplication analysis](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/analysis.md) of the parent item [Reduce duplication across Matters within a Session](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/). See [Intent](intent.md) for the pattern shape, the Matters categories it shows up in, the observed occurrences, and the quality rubric.

Closed 2026-10-29 at the user's direction, without implementation. This work is superseded by [Unified Matters Pipeline](../unified-matters-pipeline/), which replaces category-by-category Matters generation with a thread-based pipeline designed to prevent this duplication. No handling was decided for this pattern; its evidence remains reference material for the new item.

# Matters Duplication: Open Bargains Restated Across Categories

A two-sided bargain struck this Session and still unfinished when play stops is restated in several categories at once, with one category per side of the deal: Development, Goal or Obligation, Opportunity, and New Asset. Seen in three analysed Brandonsford Sessions. The goal is a Session Summary in which this repetition no longer occurs, while distinct information is preserved.

This is pattern P6 from the [duplication analysis](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/analysis.md#cross-pattern-observation-open-bargains) of the parent item [Reduce duplication across Matters within a Session](../reduce-session-matters-duplication/). It overlaps patterns P1, P2 and P5, whose individual links it contains. See [Intent](intent.md) for the pattern shape, the Matters categories it shows up in, the observed occurrences, and the quality rubric.

Closed 2026-10-29 at the user's direction, without implementation. This work is superseded by [Unified Matters Pipeline](../unified-matters-pipeline/), which replaces category-by-category Matters generation with a thread-based pipeline designed to prevent this duplication. No handling was decided for this pattern; its evidence remains reference material for the new item.

# Session Summary Quality Investigation

Understand why generated Session summaries have become longer and less useful, distinguishing prompt effects from upstream changes and generation variability. The agreed experiment and its rationale are recorded in [idea.md](idea.md).

## Quality Rubric

The user approved these dimensions for judging the investigation's quality. They judge the experiment and its conclusions; they are not a separate rubric for scoring individual summaries. Assess each dimension through qualitative judgments supported by evidence, without numerical scores, weights, anchors, or an aggregate rating.

| Dimension | What It Values |
|---|---|
| Explanatory confidence | Conclusions distinguish prompt effects, upstream changes, and generation variability, with uncertainty made clear. |
| Meaningful quality judgments | Reviews capture what makes summaries useful for future play, rather than treating shorter output as automatically better. |
| Reproducibility | Inputs, prompt revisions, settings, outputs, and observations are preserved so comparisons can be repeated and checked. |
| Practical effort | The experiment yields useful evidence without excessive preparation, generation cost, or reading. |

These qualities are independently assessable: a reproducible experiment can still draw unjustified conclusions, and a convincing finding can still require excessive effort. No evaluations have been performed and no dimension definitions remain unresolved.

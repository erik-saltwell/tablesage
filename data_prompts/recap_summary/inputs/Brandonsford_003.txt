<glossary>
- Alfred: A shaggy giant companion of Sir Brandon, depicted wielding a greatsword in the barrow mound murals.
- Bentley: Halfling owner of the Clumsy Fox Tavern.
- Brandon's Barrow: A burial mound where Brandon is supposed to be burried.
- Brandonsford: A quiet town.
- Brother Dirk: Assistant to Father William
- Clumsy Fox Tavern: A warm, busy tavern in Brandonsford owned by Bentley, a jovial halfling. Known for its Fox Tail brew.
- Clurichen: A small trickster creature that comes from the river at night and causes mischief in town.
- Dagonite: A pale-skinned, human-like race with unusual eyes.
- Drop Dead Ned: Greasy-haired assassin. Owns a killer wasp in a jar.
- Dunk: A trollkin adventurer who traded with fauns and plotted to steal Warwick's mithril shirt using Ingrid's help.
- Eric the Reeve: Landowner representative of Brandonsford. Kind of the mayor.
- Farmer Gill: Local farmer with three sons.
- Father William: Racist priest of Brandonsford's church.
- Faun wine: A red, intoxicating wine that bleeds like sap from trees in the Fauns Grove.
- Fauns Grove: A grove slightly north of Brandonsford across the river where fauns gather wine that bleeds from the trees
- Fox Tail brew: The signature ale served at the Clumsy Fox Tavern, known for its excellent taste.
- Free Goblin Society: A collective of goblins led by Snurk, Grimble, Watok, and Peg who fled King Hogboon to settle the barrow mound.
- George: One-armed woodsman of Brandonsford who encountered the black dragon and lost his arm to it.
- Giant's cottage: A cottage three times normal size where a giant lives.
- Golden Egg Tavern: A nearly empty tavern across from the Clumsy Fox, owned by Quinn.
- Grimble Who Holds the Stick: A goblin from the Free Goblin Society who insists on using a speaking stick to maintain order.
- Ingrid: The town alchemist who allegedly has romantic interest in Warwick.
- King Hogboon: A goblin king from whom the Free Goblin Society fled to establish their new collective.
- Lady Hilda: Red-haired noble woman adventurer staying at the Clumsy Fox.
- Lady Isaboob: Sir Phidipaldi's lance.
- Malzazarik the Magnificent: A wizard in purple robes and pointy hat with a yellow star, staying at the Clumsy Fox Tavern.
- Mithril chain shirt: A valuable piece of armor on display in Warwick's shop that he refuses to sell.
- Myrddin: A wizened priest companion of Sir Brandon, depicted with a staff and clasped hands in the barrow mound murals.
- Naggeneen: A Clurichen (small trickster creature) who emerges from the river at night to steal alcohol from the Golden Egg Tavern.
- Peg Who Had the Torch: A female goblin wearing a black robe and eye patch, member of the Free Goblin Society.
- Quinn: White-bearded, bony owner of the Golden Egg Tavern.
- Reginald: A frog living by the river near Brandonsford - hates Nageneen.
- Saint Arthur: Patron saint of hunters, depicted as a bearded man with a spear in religious carvings and statues.
- Seamus: Farmer Gill's shabby mule
- Sir Brandon: Legendary hero who killed a dragon and whom Brandonsford is named after.
- Sir Brandon's sword: A holy sword of silvery white metal etched with 'All shadows yield to my light,' buried with Brandon.
- Sir Chumunga: Sir Phidipaldi's two-handed sword.
- Snurk the Walkout: A mud-colored goblin and member of the Free Goblin Society who advocates for their new collective.
- Squints: A river halfling fighter with a sling.
- Trollkin: Large race not related to trolls
- Vivian: Ingrid's friend who lives in the forest.
- Warwick: The town smith who keeps his shop filled with lucky charms.
- Watok the Scribbler: A hunched goblin with ink-stained fingers who serves as scribe for the Free Goblin Society.
- Wyllt: A wild-eyed axeman companion of Sir Brandon, depicted carrying a heavy load in the barrow mound murals.

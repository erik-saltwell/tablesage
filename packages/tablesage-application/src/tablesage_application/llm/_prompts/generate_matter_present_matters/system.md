# How to write Present Matters
## Current Position
State where each group of characters is, naming the place specifically enough to recognize it. Use "the party" only when everyone is together; when the party is split, give each group its own clause. Add companions only when they are acting with the party or their presence matters. Leave out how they got there; the Scene List records that.

### Example

- Good: Mira and Tobin are in the flooded bell vault beneath the lighthouse; Corvin is on the quay with Captain Sella.
- Avoid: After crossing the harbor, the party made its way to the lighthouse and is exploring the bell vault with Sella, the ferryman, and two dockhands.

## Immediate Goals
State what the party is trying to do in the scene play stopped in, preferably beginning with an action verb. Add the obstacle in a brief clause only when the goal alone doesn't show what stands in the way. Name the characters pursuing it when it isn't the whole party. Keep to the current scene; a larger aim that the scene serves belongs in the Goals open matter. Describe the goal without recommending methods.

### Example

- Good: Get the ferryman to row them across the harbor; he refuses to sail after dark.
- Avoid: Recover the tide bell. First the party must cross the harbor, but the ferryman refuses to sail after dark, so they could bribe him or take his boat.

## Present Danger
State the danger and whom it threatens, including any established sign of how close or severe it is. Preserve uncertainty; do not predict the outcome or raise the stakes beyond what was established. An ongoing threat may remain in Brewing Troubles after the immediate danger ends; describe only the immediate danger here. Describe the danger without suggesting how to escape or counter it.

### Example

- Good: Seawater is pouring into the bell vault, and Tobin is still pinned under a fallen rack.
- Avoid: The vault will flood within minutes and Tobin will drown unless Mira cuts him free.

# Supplied category context
Use prior Present Matters for continuity, then reconcile each group against the current Scene Log. State the stopping situation, not old positions or completed actions.

# Output
Return only {"present_matters": ...} matching the supplied schema. Use an empty array for an empty list. Do not output any other category.

# Scene log: brandonsford-002

**Session cue:** "Leave Naggeneen alone! I just like to get ripped" - Naggeneen

## 1. Dunk outdrinking Farmer Gill on the porch at sunset
**Farmer Gill's farm:** Dunk accepted Farmer Gill's free barn bed and haggled unsuccessfully over Seamus the mule.

**Events**
- [medium] Dunk tried to talk the price of Seamus the mule down to 20 gold, but Gill refused for now: without his boys or a mule he can't get any work done, so he will only drop the price once his boys come back.
- **Outcome:** Dunk settled on staying in Gill's barn; the mule purchase was left waiting on the return of Gill's sons.

## 2. "Back home I could bullseye womp rats" - Squints
**The Clumsy Fox:** Trout befriended Squints, a halfling seeking an adventuring crew, and invited him to meet in the morning.

**Events**
- [medium] Dr. Uriah, who carries the party's physical map, recalled nothing of its lore or history and planned to secretly copy it in his downtime.
- [medium] Sir Phidipaldi could only sleep at the Golden Egg because he had been thrown out of the Clumsy Fox for starting a fight with the mayor; Trout and Dr. Uriah stayed at the Clumsy Fox at 10 silver a night.
- [medium] Squints said he had just come to town looking for his big break and wanted to join an adventuring crew, showing off his sling.
- [medium] Trout told Squints to meet him there next morning to be introduced to his friends, deferring the question of pay as too early; Squints agreed.
- **Outcome:** Trout arranged a morning meeting with Squints, and the party split up for the night.

## 3. "Leave Naggeneen alone! I just like to get ripped" - Naggeneen
**The Golden Egg's basement:** The party caught Naggeneen the Clurichen stealing Quinn's alcohol, and Quinn paid them each 33 gold.

**Events**
- [medium] Sir Phidipaldi slept in the Golden Egg's basement, where Quinn keeps his alcohol, to catch the thief, with Quinn having promised to pay him for it.
- [medium] Sir Phidipaldi covered the basement floor with his bag of marbles, and the invisible thief slipped on them, landed on its back and became visible.
- [medium] Dr. Uriah and Trout kicked the door open; Uriah threw vials of ink to reveal the thief while Trout threw his bag of fish over it and trapped it.
- [medium] Naggeneen begged to be released, offering his pot of gold or a secret, and Sir Phidipaldi refused, saying he was all honor and chivalry and did not need the secret.
- [medium] Quinn came down asking whether they had finally caught Bentley red-handed, and Sir Phidipaldi told him the thief was not who he thought.
- [high] Quinn said he knew what he would do with Naggeneen, hung the flailing bag on a hook behind the bar, and paid Sir Phidipaldi, Trout and Dr. Uriah 33 gold each.
- **Outcome:** The thief was caught and the reward paid, with Naggeneen left hanging in the bag behind Quinn's bar.

**Discoveries**
- [high] Naggeneen, a Clurichen, was the one stealing and drinking Quinn's alcohol, not Bentley as Quinn had suspected.
- [medium] Naggeneen claims to be a good luck spirit and says he has a pot of gold and a secret he would trade for his freedom.

## 4. Rabbit feet and clovers nailed all over the smithy walls
**Warwick's smithy:** Dunk spotted a magical mithril chain shirt in Warwick's closed smithy and followed a startled woman.

**Events**
- [medium] A thin, frazzled woman heading for the house was spooked by Dunk and walked off as if she hadn't been going there, and Dunk followed her.
- **Outcome:** Dunk left the smithy following the woman into town.

**Discoveries**
- [high] Warwick's shop holds iron daggers, a longsword, a lot of junk, and a luminous mithril chain shirt clearly of magical origin.
- [medium] Warwick sleeps on the second floor, where the window is left cracked open.

## 5. Tiny invisible footprints in the reeds
**The river:** Trout and Dr. Uriah tracked the invisible Clurichen from the river to the Golden Egg.

**Events**
- [medium] The Clurichen swam across the river dragging a corked jug, hauled it onto the bank and turned invisible.
- [medium] Dr. Uriah and Trout each followed its small footprints through the reeds to the Golden Egg, where the basement door flung open and closed with no one visible.
- **Outcome:** Trout and Dr. Uriah followed the Clurichen to the Golden Egg's basement door.

**Discoveries**
- [medium] The Clurichen crosses the river at night dragging a jug and can turn invisible, though it still leaves footprints.

## 6. Ingrid's nightly gifts on Warwick's windowsill
**Ingrid's home:** Dunk agreed to fetch faun wine so Ingrid can brew a sleeping potion for Warwick.

**Events**
- [medium] When Dunk asked about her visit to Warwick's, Ingrid blushed, and Dunk lied that Warwick had mentioned a beautiful apothecary.
- [medium] Ingrid asked the tall Dunk to place her gifts on Warwick's windowsill for her, since climbing makes her nervous.
- [medium] Dunk proposed she brew a potion to calm her nerves, he lift her to the window, and she unlock the front door so he could get in; he claimed to want inside because he is a fan of clovers, and Ingrid grew dubious and declined.
- [high] Dunk then proposed she prove her skill by brewing a sleeping potion, which he would help her get inside and give to Warwick so she could watch him all night without fear.
- [high] Dunk agreed to see what he could do about the faun wine and to meet her at the same spot and time the next night.
- [high] Ingrid thanked Dunk for keeping her romance secret and said she owed him; Dunk replied, 'I haven't kept it a secret yet,' and she looked scared.
- **Outcome:** Dunk left committed to fetching faun wine, with a meeting set for the next night.

**Discoveries**
- [high] Ingrid is secretly in love with Warwick: every night she climbs up to leave little gifts on his windowsill and watches him sleep, but she has never spoken to him.
- [high] Ingrid most fears that Warwick will wake and learn she watches him through his window every night; she says she would die of shame.
- [high] Ingrid says she knows how to make a sleeping potion but needs a key ingredient, wine from the Faun Grove, plus one other unnamed ingredient.
- [high] The Faun Grove lies a little across the river north of town in the woods; its trees bleed red wine that the fauns collect, and the wine can make any creature drunk or put it to sleep. It is close enough to visit in a day.

## 7. Squints's 'junior assistant halfling' title, three tips a day
**The Clumsy Fox:** Trout recruited Squints as junior assistant halfling in the party's Halfling Corps.

**Events**
- [high] Trout offered Squints the post of 'junior assistant halfling' in the Halfling Corps he leads: Squints funds himself and his own gear until the first treasure, elite members take first pickings and Squints second, and he takes orders directly from Trout.
- [high] Squints joined the party, starry-eyed at his big break, armed with a sling and dagger and more experienced than Trout.
- [medium] Trout told Squints to follow him, observe and not talk much, allowing him up to three tips a day; Squints agreed.
- **Outcome:** Squints joined the party under Trout's command.

## 8. Father William measuring Uriah's hands and feet with a pocket ruler
**The church:** Father William told Dr. Uriah he sent Brother Dirk to the Barrow Mound for Brandon's sword.

**Events**
- [medium] Dr. Uriah visited the church out of antiquarian curiosity and met Father William, who is racist toward nonhumans.
- [medium] Father William said he had no books for Uriah but offered to heal him if injured.
- [high] When Uriah asked him to mark the Barrow Mound and the coven on the map, Father William asked him to give penance at the Barrow Mound and donate 10 gold for its upkeep; Uriah agreed and let him measure his feet.
- [high] Father William found no webbing, muttered that he may have misheard, and marked the witch's home and the Barrow Mound on Uriah's map.
- **Outcome:** Dr. Uriah left with the Barrow Mound and the witch's home marked on his map.

**Discoveries**
- [high] Father William says he sent Brother Dirk a while ago to kill the dragon, first going to Brandon's Barrow Mound, because the dragon can't be killed without the magic sword buried with Brandon.
- [medium] Father William says the Barrow Mound is about a day's walk away.
- [medium] Father William claims Ingrid is a 'little pagan' who isn't competent and can't make her potions herself, and that she and the witch in the woods run a coven with lots of scrolls.

## 9. George chopping off his own arm after the dragon's bite
**The Woodsman's Hut:** One-armed George described the Black Dragon's lair and deadly abilities to Sir Phidipaldi.

**Events**
- [medium] Sir Phidipaldi asked what he could do in return; George said killing the dragon would be great, though he doubted Phidipaldi could, and suggested a lance might be one of the few weapons not yet tried.
- [medium] Sir Phidipaldi promised to bring his lance.
- **Outcome:** Sir Phidipaldi left with knowledge of the dragon and a promise to bring his lance.

**Discoveries**
- [high] George says the Black Dragon's lair is up north at the foot of the mountains, reachable without climbing; it stays there because it can't fly yet, but George says it will soon, and then they're doomed.
- [high] George says the dragon killed all his friends; its jaws drip poison and one bite kills, which is why he had to chop off his arm; it spits sulfur smelling of rotten eggs; and nothing pierced its black scales, neither arrows nor swords.
- [high] George doubts Eric actually has the 1000 gold he promised for killing the dragon.
- [medium] George says the only one ever to kill the dragon was Sir Brandon, 1000 or 2000 years ago; schoolchildren are taught a fairy gave Brandon a magic sword. No one knows what is true, George doesn't know where the sword is, and Farmer Gill knows the fairy tales.

## 10. The fauns gobsmacked as Dunk drinks their wine unaffected
**The Faun Grove:** Dunk traded a child's tear-stained rabbit's foot to the fauns for a flask of faun wine.

**Events**
- [medium] Dunk joined the fauns' dancing and drank their wine, and also their 'emergency wine' from neck flasks, without any effect, astonishing them.
- [high] Dunk traded a crusty, tear-stained rabbit's foot that had kept a child in his village safe, and a delighted faun gave him her necklace flask of faun wine.
- **Outcome:** Dunk left the grove with a flask of faun wine for Ingrid's potion.

**Discoveries**
- [medium] The fauns say their wine makes almost every creature drunk immediately, except fauns; the 'emergency wine' in their neck flasks is the same wine.
- [medium] The fauns collect sentimental items and will trade their wine for things like a love letter or a pixie's treasure.

## 11. Ingrid and Vivian 'reclaiming the word' witch
**Ingrid's shop:** Dr. Uriah bonded with Ingrid over disliking Father William and bought wolfsbane and healing tea.

**Events**
- [medium] When Uriah relayed Father William's claim that she is a front, Ingrid insisted she makes all her wares herself.
- [medium] Dr. Uriah bought essence of wolfsbane (5 gold) and mandrake root tea that heals wounds (10 gold), passing on witch powder (7 gold).
- **Outcome:** Dr. Uriah left with his purchases and headed back to the tavern.

**Discoveries**
- [medium] Ingrid says things have changed lately: the dragon is around and fairies that used to stay hidden are encroaching more and more.
- [medium] Ingrid's friend Vivian lives in the forest; asked if Vivian is a witch, Ingrid said they are trying to reclaim the word.

## 12. "Whoa, look at you, you're huge" - Squints
**The Clumsy Fox:** The party pooled dragon intelligence and planned to head to the Barrow Mound.

**Events**
- [high] Dunk said Warwick has shimmering armor that might help against the dragon and he has a plan to get it, needing one more night before rejoining; he refused to tell Squints the plan.
- [medium] Sir Phidipaldi shared George's account of the dragon's poison bite, sulfur and impenetrable scales, and that the sword is at the Barrow.
- [medium] Trout asked whether there was treasure and suggested they go to the Barrow.
- [medium] Dr. Uriah warned the others that the priest is a racist and won't like them; Trout noted Brother Dirk was headed to the Barrow, and Sir Phidipaldi added that Farmer Gill's sons are after the dragon too.
- [high] Trout proposed going on foot, skipping the witch's house to the south, checking a map location to the north that may be on the way, and telling Dunk to meet them at the Barrow.
- **Outcome:** The party settled on heading north toward the Barrow Mound.

**Discoveries**
- [medium] By the map, the party's planned route is roughly a three-mile walk through the forest.

## 13. Faun wine cut to 20% with tavern wine
**Ingrid's shop:** Dunk gave Ingrid heavily diluted faun wine so she could start the sleeping potion.

**Events**
- [high] Dunk diluted the faun wine to about 20% faun wine and 80% tavern wine, leaving it strong enough that Ingrid would recognize the smell.
- [high] Dunk gave the diluted wine to Ingrid so she could start the potion while he was away, telling her he'd see her tonight; Ingrid said she would work on it and he should come back tomorrow.
- **Outcome:** Ingrid set to work on the potion, with Dunk due back to collect it.

## 14. Trout's sling stone hitting Squints in the head
**The giant's cottage:** Squints snuck into a sleeping giant's cottage and rescued Brother Dirk from a sack.

**Events**
- [medium] Dunk rejoined the others, and the whole party with Squints headed north through the woods to a giant-sized cottage of Cyclopean rocks with a horrible droning roar inside.
- [medium] Trout boosted Squints to a high window, and Squints reported a sleeping giant with a big, human-sized wiggling sack hanging on the wall beside a pot of cooking food.
- [medium] Trout told Squints this was his big test: fetch the sack, which he said held members of another party out to beat the dragon, with promised intervention if caught; privately Trout told the others they wouldn't rescue Squints, and Sir Phidipaldi agreed, 'Definitely not.'
- [high] Squints crept in, froze when the giant stirred, and pulled the sack down, releasing a bound and gagged Brother Dirk as the giant bolted upright shouting, 'Who goes there?'
- [medium] Dr. Uriah smashed the window to distract the giant while Squints and Brother Dirk escaped through the door Dunk and Sir Phidipaldi opened.
- [medium] The slow giant chased them for a while before tiring, and the party easily outran it.
- [high] Brother Dirk thanked them, said he had been headed to the Barrow for the sword to save the town, and said he should just head home now; Dr. Uriah asked what Father William would say, since the priest insisted the dragon was handled because he had sent Dirk.
- [medium] Sir Phidipaldi told Dirk the party was headed to the Barrow too, and Trout told Dirk he would be reporting to Squints, who was pleased to have a follower; Dirk's answer was not recorded.
- **Outcome:** The party escaped the giant with Brother Dirk rescued, his plans uncertain, still on the way to the Barrow.

**Discoveries**
- [medium] Brother Dirk says the giant was making a stew and planned to eat him in it.

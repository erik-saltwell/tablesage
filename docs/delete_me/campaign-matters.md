# Campaign Matters: brandonsford-002

## Scene List
1. Dunk accepted Farmer Gill's free barn bed and haggled unsuccessfully over Seamus the mule.
2. Trout befriended Squints, a halfling seeking an adventuring crew, and invited him to meet in the morning.
3. The party caught Naggeneen the Clurichen stealing Quinn's alcohol, and Quinn paid them each 33 gold.
4. Dunk spotted a magical mithril chain shirt in Warwick's closed smithy and followed a startled woman.
5. Trout and Dr. Uriah tracked the invisible Clurichen from the river to the Golden Egg.
6. Dunk agreed to fetch faun wine so Ingrid can brew a sleeping potion for Warwick.
7. Trout recruited Squints as junior assistant halfling in the party's Halfling Corps.
8. Father William told Dr. Uriah he sent Brother Dirk to the Barrow Mound for Brandon's sword.
9. One-armed George described the Black Dragon's lair and deadly abilities to Sir Phidipaldi.
10. Dunk traded a child's tear-stained rabbit's foot to the fauns for a flask of faun wine.
11. Dr. Uriah bonded with Ingrid over disliking Father William and bought wolfsbane and healing tea.
12. The party pooled dragon intelligence and planned to head to the Barrow Mound.
13. Dunk gave Ingrid heavily diluted faun wine so she could start the sleeping potion.
14. Squints snuck into a sleeping giant's cottage and rescued Brother Dirk from a sack.

## Revelations
- [low] Farmer Gill won't lower the price of Seamus the mule until his sons come back. Without them or a mule he can't get any work done.
- [high] Naggeneen, a Clurichen, was the one stealing and drinking Quinn's ale, not Bentley as Quinn suspected. He comes across the river at night with a jug and turns invisible, though he still leaves footprints.
- [low] Naggeneen says he is a good luck spirit and has a pot of gold and a secret he would trade for his freedom.
- [high] Warwick's shop holds a glowing mithril chain shirt that is clearly magical.
- [medium] Warwick sleeps on the second floor of his smithy and leaves the window cracked open.
- [high] Ingrid the Alchemist is secretly in love with Warwick. Every night she climbs up to leave small gifts on his windowsill and watch him sleep, but she has never spoken to him. Her biggest fear is that he'll wake up and find out; she says she would die of shame.
- [medium] Ingrid's sleeping potion needs wine from the Faun Grove plus one other ingredient she didn't name.
- [low] The Faun Grove is in the woods across the river north of town, close enough to reach in a day. Its trees bleed wine that the fauns collect.
- [medium] Faun wine makes almost any creature drunk right away or puts it to sleep. It doesn't work on fauns.
- [low] The fauns collect sentimental items and will trade their wine for things like a love letter or a pixie's treasure.
- [high] Father William says the dragon can't be killed without the magic sword buried with Sir Brandon in his Barrow Mound. He sent Brother Dirk there some time ago to get the sword and kill the dragon.
- [low] Father William claims Ingrid can't make her own potions and runs a coven with the witch in the woods. Ingrid insists she makes all her wares herself.
- [high] The Black Dragon's lair is up north at the foot of the mountains, and you can reach it without climbing. It stays there because it can't fly yet. George says it will fly soon, and then the town is doomed.
- [high] The Black Dragon's bite is poisonous and one bite kills; George cut off his own arm because of it. The dragon also spits sulfur that smells like rotten eggs, and neither arrows nor swords have pierced its black scales.
- [medium] George doubts that Eric the Reeve actually has the 1,000 gold he promised for killing the dragon.
- [medium] George says the story of a fairy giving Sir Brandon a magic sword is just what schoolchildren are taught. He says nobody knows what's true and he doesn't know where the sword is.
- [low] The giant in the stone cottage north of town is very slow and gets tired quickly when it chases someone.

**Immediate Goals**
- Get Brother Dirk to come along; he said he should just head home, and he hasn't answered Trout's order that he report to Squints.
- Continue north through the forest to the Barrow Mound.

**Present Danger**
- None.

## Open Matters

### Goals
- Find the treasure marked on the party's treasure map.
- Kill the Black Dragon near Brandonsford and collect Eric the Reeve's 1,000 gold bounty.
- Dunk is considering buying Farmer Gill's mule, Seamus.
- Dr. Uriah is considering hiring Drop Dead Ned.
- Dr. Uriah plans to secretly copy the party's map.

### Unresolved Mysteries
- Where is the treasure on the party's treasure map? The map marks several spots near Brandonsford that the party doesn't recognize. Trout wants to check one to the north that may be on the way to the Barrow Mound.
- Why have shipments stopped reaching Brandonsford? Bentley's restock is overdue, Farmer Gill says nothing has come into town in months, and shipments still hadn't arrived the next morning.
- Is Brandon's magic sword really buried at the Barrow Mound, and is it the only way to kill the Black Dragon? Father William says so, but George says it's only a fairy tale that a fairy gave Brandon the sword, no one knows what's true, and he doesn't know where it is. He says Farmer Gill knows the old tales.
- Does Eric the Reeve actually have the 1,000 gold he promised for killing the dragon? George doubts it.
- Why have the fairies that used to stay hidden been coming closer and closer to town lately? Ingrid says things have changed recently, with the dragon around and the fairies pushing in.

### Outstanding Obligations
- Sir Phidipaldi agreed to help Quinn of the Golden Egg get back at Bentley by staging a fake ale delivery: Quinn brings up bottles, they fill them with water, and Sir Phidipaldi makes a big show of delivering ale to Quinn's cellar. Quinn offered 100 gold for it. The thief turned out to be Naggeneen, not Bentley, but the deal hasn't been called off.
- Dr. Uriah promised Father William he would do penance at the Barrow Mound and give 10 gold for its upkeep, in return for Father William marking the Barrow Mound and the witch's home on his map.
- Sir Phidipaldi promised George the Hunter he would bring his lance to try against the Black Dragon.
- Dunk is due back at Ingrid's to collect the sleeping potion she's brewing from the faun wine he brought her, which he watered down to about 20% strength. Under his plan, he then helps her get into Warwick's house and gives Warwick the potion so she can watch him all night without fear.

### Developing Threats
- A black dragon is terrorizing Brandonsford from its lair up north at the foot of the mountains. George says it can't fly yet but will soon, and then the town is doomed. He also says its bite is poisonous and kills, it spits sulfur, and arrows and swords can't pierce its scales. Father William claims it can't be killed without the magic sword buried with Brandon.
- No shipments have reached Brandonsford in a long time, and they still haven't come. Bentley's restock is overdue, and Warwick the smith has almost nothing to sell. Bentley says the shortage affects the whole town.
- A giant lives in a stone cottage in the woods north of town, on the party's route toward the Barrow Mound. It was going to cook Brother Dirk in a stew, and it chased the party after they rescued him. It is slow and tires quickly.

### Available Opportunities
- Lady Hilda will join a party of adventurers hunting the dragon for money, though she won't hunt it alone.
- Drop Dead Ned and his killer wasp are available to Dr. Uriah for 100 gold for the week, and he'll do anything as long as he gets to kill people. He'll pay Dr. Uriah 5 gold back for every person he's allowed to kill and do what he wants with.
- If you free Naggeneen from the bag hanging behind Quinn's bar at the Golden Egg, he says he'll give you his pot of gold or a secret.
- If you retrieve Brandon's magic sword from the Barrow Mound, about a day's walk away, it may let you kill the Black Dragon. Father William says the dragon can't be killed without it, but George says the sword's story may only be a fairy tale.
- Farmer Gill knows the old tales about Sir Brandon and his fairy-given sword, according to George. Asking him could tell you more about the sword and how Brandon killed the dragon.
- Warwick sleeps on the second floor of his smithy and leaves the window cracked open. That gives you a way into the house, where his shop holds a magical mithril chain shirt. Dunk thinks the shirt might help against the dragon.
- Once Ingrid finishes her sleeping potion, it could put Warwick to sleep. She made it from faun wine that Dunk watered down to about 20% strength.
- The fauns at the Faun Grove trade their wine for sentimental items like a love letter or a pixie's treasure. Their wine makes almost any creature except fauns drunk right away or puts it to sleep.
- Father William offered to heal Dr. Uriah if he gets hurt.
